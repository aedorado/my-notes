# The Dual Mounts of Mars
### Maṅgala Parvata · Āntarika aur Bāhya Śaurya

Mars is unusual among the mounts in occupying two distinct locations on the hand rather than one. **Lower Mars (nimna maṅgala)** sits near the thumb, between Jupiter and Venus; **Upper Mars (ucca maṅgala)** sits under the area of the Moon and Mercury, a little lower than those mounts. Because of these differing neighbours, reading either Mars always requires reading the surrounding mounts alongside it.

## 1. Foundational Principles & Anatomy

Mars occupies **two places** on the hand, each with its own name, location, and neighbours.

| Position | Hindi | Location | Neighbours |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Lower Mars** (nimna maṅgala) | निम्न मंगल | Near the thumb | Between Jupiter and Venus |
| **Upper Mars** (ucca maṅgala) | उच्च मंगल | Under the Moon–Mercury area, a little lower | Together with Mercury and Chandra (Moon) |

Because of these neighbours, whenever Mars is being predicted, the surrounding mounts — Jupiter and Venus for Lower Mars; Mercury and Moon for Upper Mars — must be read together with it.

**Reading Lower Mars** calls for three things to be kept in mind together: the condition of the surrounding mounts, especially Jupiter and particularly Venus; the colour of the area — whether it looks blackish or bluish; and any signs or symbols present (reserved for later study, since they are hard to identify precisely in this area). The number of lines clustering here also matters — nearly everyone has many lines in this region — and comparing the thumb open versus closed, or comparing both hands against each other, helps clarify how raised or depressed the area truly is in a given hand.

Lines rising from Lower Mars are called **enemy lines** — astrologically, what is read from the 6th house corresponds to what is read here. The more such lines rise and cut across the Life Line, Heart Line, or Mind Line, the more they impact that specific area of life at the corresponding age: the heart-related effect appears at a later age, but the mind and health effects tend to show at an early age, around 10–15 years. A hand can look entirely plain and still belong to someone under great tension, in which case the clusters present in this area, and the colour of the lines (light versus dark), become the deciding factors.

## 2. Structural Variations — Lower vs. Upper Mars

The **Upper Mars** can appear raised, protruding slightly outward to the side, while the middle "Rahu area" between the two Mars zones may appear a little depressed. One example hand shows Lower Mars raised while Upper Mars is depressed — demonstrating that the two need not move together.

| | Lower Mars (nimna) | Upper Mars (ucca) |
|---|---|---|
| Nature | Inner (āntarika) strength — to *feel* courage | Outer (bāhya) — to *show* courage |
| Shows | Inner courage, will power, strategy forming in the mind | Ability to display retaliation (pratiśodha), act outwardly |
| Weak means | Trembling inside | Cannot show it outside |

> [!CASE] Case Example — Courage Felt vs. Courage Shown
> Consider someone alone at home during a power cut who goes out to arrange a light — but who, inside, is actually trembling. That combination shows a bad Lower Mars alongside a good Upper Mars. Upper Mars is specifically the capacity to *show* outward courage or retaliation; soldiers and police, for instance, need both inner and outer Mars together, since they require courage both felt and displayed. One who can show courage can even come to blows or pick up a stick to fight; one who can only feel it inside will only ever feel it.

> [!CASE] Case Example — A Fully Depressed Outer Mars
> One example hand showed a fully depressed outer Mars: the person will not personally kill even a lizard or cockroach that enters the house, instead asking a family member to do it, since that family member's Mars is in a very good condition.

## 3. States of Development

### Upper (Outer) Mars

In the **over-developed** condition, a very raised Upper Mars shows as considerable anger, visible at a glance — a quality that can be genuinely needed in fields such as border duty (illustrated by the extraordinary on-screen strength of a Sunny Deol-type action role), but which, outside such a suited context (deśa-kāla), vents instead at home through breaking things. Such people may include those capable of murder or theft, and, with Venus also in bad condition, potentially a rapist; the recommended approach where this tendency appears without a suitable outlet is to channel the excess energy into structured activity, such as regular exercise.

In the **normal** condition, everything is shown within a limit — the person knows where to show anger, carries patience (dhairya) and courage (sāhasa), and understands responsibility, illustrated by soldiers on the border who endure isolation for months with great patience while still fulfilling their duty. This is considered a very good condition, and such a person knows how to control anger — provided the thumb is also checked, since the thumb can itself become a separate cause of anger. Generosity (udāratā) is a hallmark trait here: a person with normal Upper Mars protects others without regard for their own safety, and without regard for caste or religion. An over-developed Upper Mars, in summary, is sometimes good and sometimes bad depending entirely on place, time, and situation; the normal condition carries all of Mars's good qualities without that ambiguity.

In the **low** condition, the person becomes a coward, unable even to deliver a necessary corrective slap to a misbehaving child, and is so fearful they lack the capacity to speak up at all. Because Moon and Mercury sit on either side of this mount, a very good Mercury condition (good lines, good finger) in such a person produces someone expert at speaking — fine for giving speeches — but who cannot translate that speech into action when it is actually required.

### Lower (Inner) Mars

Lower Mars reads the enthusiasm (utsāha), courage, and inner strength a person feels. Because it sits near Venus, desires and affairs are also discussed from this area, with specific lines, signs, and symbols (reserved for later study) showing affairs and inner mental state.

A **low or troubled Lower Mars** shows through very many lines, a dark, bluish, or slightly reddish colour — understood as possible blood-related trouble, including low hemoglobin. Such a person can produce reasoning for everything, turning ordinary matters into a pattern of constant justification; confidence and enthusiasm both run low when the condition is poor, and difficulty fulfilling physical needs can arise, read specifically from a married-life perspective.

An **excessively raised** Lower Mars can be both good and bad, producing increased desires in the mind — sometimes unnecessary ones, given its proximity to Venus. A **fully depressed** Lower Mars is not good at all: it damages health first and then the mindset, turning desires toward the wrong kind, and producing a characteristic pre-competition or pre-exam fear — "my exam is not going to go well; nothing will happen for me today."

| Mount | Over-developed | Normal | Low/Depressed |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Upper (Outer) Mars** | Excessive anger; good or bad depending on context; can indicate capacity for violence if combined with poor Venus | Controlled anger, patience, responsibility, generosity, protects others selflessly | Cowardice; cannot act even when able to speak well (if Mercury is good) |
| **Lower (Inner) Mars** | Excessive/unnecessary desire due to Venus proximity; can be good or bad | Genuine enthusiasm, will power, inner strategy | Damaged health and mindset; wrong-direction desires; fear before challenges; possible blood-related trouble |

> [!CAUTION] Caution
> Context (deśa-kāla) must always be read alongside an over-developed Upper Mars before judging it as purely negative — the very quality that would be destructive at home can be exactly what is needed in a role such as border duty or policing, where both inner and outer Mars are required together.

## 4. Signs, Markings & Practical Indications

> [!CASE] Case Example — A Female Hand with Weak Will Power
> In one example hand, the thumb's first phalanx was long but not plump (indicating a lack of will power), with many lines on the middle phalanx suggesting poor thumb condition overall. Venus was read as: despite having everything, this person cannot enjoy its pleasures in life. No mount was in very good condition, Mars included, and the overall hand looked thin and weak, as though recovering from illness — yet because the hand was fiery, and being female (and therefore, per the reading given, unlikely to break rules and regulations), hesitation in speaking was predicted rather than overt rule-breaking.

> [!CASE] Case Example — A Male Hand with a Dense Venus Jālī
> In a second example, a male hand's Venus mount had plenty of space but was not in good condition — somewhat pressed, with a clearly net-like (jālī) cluster of lines beyond the ordinary net found on most hands. The rule drawn from this case: if lines appear very dark at first glance, this shows struggle in life; lines should ideally appear a little lighter rather than thick and dark.

> [!CASE] Case Example — Chart and Hand in Agreement
> On one hand, inner Mars was assessed as not in good condition at present, while outer Mars was only normal — certainly not raised. This person's chart showed Venus in Tulā (Libra) but combust (asta), and that chart condition was indeed visible in the corresponding hand reading. A visible line on this hand was confirmed as an "enemy line" that had been present for some time across two separate photos.

> [!CASE] Case Example — Divergent Mars, Again
> On another hand (identified by two moles, one on each hand), outer Mars was not good, while inner Mars and Venus were both good — a different combination again from the cases above, underlining that inner and outer Mars must always be checked independently of one another.

> [!NOTE] Chart Strength vs. Hand-Level Courage
> One chart showed Mars in its own sign (swa-rāśi) in an Aquarius ascendant, yet the corresponding hand still showed outer-Mars weakness. The two are compatible: inner and outer Mars on the hand reflect a courage-related dimension distinct from sign-based chart strength.

## 6. Sanskrit Terms Introduced

- **Maṅgala Parvata**: the Mars mount — occurring in two separate locations on the hand.
- **Nimna Maṅgala**: Lower Mars, between Jupiter and Venus, reading inner (āntarika) courage.
- **Ucca Maṅgala**: Upper Mars, under the Moon–Mercury area, reading outer (bāhya) courage.
- **Āntarika / Bāhya**: inner and outer — the two dimensions of courage read from the two Mars positions.
- **Utsāha**: enthusiasm, a core significance of Lower Mars.
- **Sāhasa**: courage, read through both Mars positions together with patience (dhairya).
- **Udāratā**: generosity, a hallmark of a normal Upper Mars.
- **Pratiśodha**: retaliation, the outward display governed by Upper Mars.
- **Deśa-Kāla**: time and place/context, essential to judging whether an over-developed Upper Mars is favourable or harmful.
- **Enemy lines**: lines rising from Lower Mars, corresponding astrologically to the 6th house.

## 7. Diagnostic Self-Check

1. Is Lower Mars being read for inner, felt courage, and Upper Mars for outer, displayed courage — and has each been checked independently, since one can be good while the other is poor?
2. For Lower Mars, has the colour (blackish, bluish, reddish) and the density/darkness of its line clusters been checked, since these point toward possible blood-related health concerns?
3. If Upper Mars appears over-developed, has the person's actual context (deśa-kāla) — a field like security or the military versus an ordinary domestic setting — been considered before judging the trait as purely negative?
4. Do any "enemy lines" from Lower Mars cut across the Life, Heart, or Mind Lines, and at what approximate age would that intersection's effect be expected?
5. Has the thumb been checked alongside Upper Mars, since the thumb itself can be an independent source of anger regardless of the mount's own condition?

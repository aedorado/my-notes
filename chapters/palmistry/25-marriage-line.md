# The Marriage Line
### Vivāha Rekhā · Śīla-Guṇa Rekhā — Timing, Forms, Yogas & Defects

The marriage line (vivāha rekhā), also called the śīla-guṇa rekhā (character-quality line) or Line of Union, is a short mark on the Mercury mount, read for the quality, timing, and durability of a person's closest bond. Per the books, it was originally meant for mental and physical bonding in general — the name "marriage" was given to it later — so its true subject is attachment itself, in whatever form deśa-kāla-paristhiti (place, time, and circumstance) gives it: marriage, a committed relationship, or a live-in arrangement.

:::glance
::::left
### IN ONE MINUTE
◆ **No line does not mean no marriage**: an absent line only suggests the person is not fully committed or emotionally connected to a partner — there are many other conditions for marriage besides this line.
◆ **More than one line does not mean two marriages**: extra lines more often show attraction, a crush, or relationships before/after marriage — only the longest, clearest, darkest line is read as *the* marriage line.
◆ **Never read it alone**: the Heart Line, the Jupiter and Venus mounts, the fate line, and the family ring must always be combined with the marriage line before any prediction is given.
◆ **Never state an extramarital affair from the hand** — this is a firm boundary of the reading.

::::right
### QUICK FACTS
- **Seat**: Budha Parvata (Mercury Mount); strongly modified by Guru (Jupiter) and Śukra (Venus)
- **Sanskrit Nomenclature**: Vivāha Rekhā; Śīla-Guṇa Rekhā; also called the Line of Union
- **Anatomical Location**: between the Heart Line and the saṃdhi rekhā (joint crease) of the last phalange of the Mercury finger
- **Age Scale**: 0 at the Heart Line to roughly 56 at the start of the Mercury finger's first phalange (formerly scaled to 50; now adjusted for later marriages, per deśa-kāla)
- **Primary Karakatvas**: quality and depth of emotional/physical bonding, timing of marriage, number of significant attachments, divorce and widowhood indications
- **Health/Household Indicators (if taught)**: a very dark line can mean energy is consumed by a household problem (illness at home); a mole on the line or on the Mercury Mount is never a good sign

## 1. Foundational Principles & Anatomy

The marriage line is located on the Mercury mount, entering from the outer edge of the hand toward the inside — it is typically very small. Before reading it, several general principles govern how any mount or finger is to be weighed. The whole hand must always be seen first, but between finger and mount, the **mount's influence is taken as slightly greater** — because the energy of the navagrahas (nine planets) is believed to be received through the fingertips and then collect in the mounts, so the mount's condition should ideally be the better of the two.

Finger and mount tilts are read the same way a conjunction (yuti) of planets is read in astrology: whichever planet's finger or mount moves toward another, the two planets' significations are read together. A **Jupiter finger tilted toward Saturn** brings research into one's knowledge, slows it down without hurry, and keeps the person from breaking rules or imposing their knowledge on others. A **Saturn finger tilted toward Jupiter** brings intelligence and discretion into one's karma, and critically, Saturn's inherent negativity does not increase — the person instead draws something positive even from a difficult situation. The same logic governs the Sun and Saturn fingers: if the **question concerns career**, a Sun finger tilted toward Saturn is very good for it; if the **question concerns name and fame**, the same tilt shows delay, a slight defame yoga, and some health trouble — the reading always follows the question being asked, not a single fixed meaning for the shift.

This question-dependent reading extends to the **Apex** as well — the same centre-point concept already established for the Jupiter and Saturn mounts (see *The Mount of Jupiter* and *The Mount of Saturn*). An apex exactly centred shows that planet's predominance in the person; all apexes centred shows an equal, ṣaḍbala-like balance of all planets; and when a mount has shifted, its apex usually shifts with it — a mount moving while its apex stays behind is said to be very rare. When the Jupiter mount tilts toward Saturn but the apex itself stays on Jupiter, predominance remains with Jupiter (seriousness, love of giving and receiving knowledge, a liking for sweet food), while the *way of working* carries Saturn's involvement.

For predicting nature broadly, the order is: **hand type** first (fiery, earthy, watery, airy — accounting for roughly 60% of the prediction), then the **thumb**, especially its top phalange (will and decision-making power), then **Mercury** (communication), and only then the finer mounts and tilts layered on top.

> [!CAUTION]
> **Caution**: do not confuse a crooked Saturn finger (teḍhī-mēḍhī) with a knotty hand. A crooked finger brings trouble in career, or a career where cleverness must be used. A knotty hand — raised knots at the finger joints, seen clearly from the back of the hand — makes a person philosophical and research-minded instead; it is an entirely separate feature.

## 2. Structural Variations & Timing on the Line

The marriage line's age scale runs along the Mercury mount between two fixed points: **zero at the Heart Line**, and the full span (formerly 50, now generally taken as **56**) at the joint crease marking the start of the Mercury finger's first phalange. Because marriages are happening later in most places today, this scale is treated as a live research subject that must keep adjusting with deśa-kāla.

A marriage line found **very close to the Heart Line** — calculating to around 12–13 years of age — signals either an early relationship/attraction or, in contexts where it genuinely happens, an early marriage. If such a line appears around 15–20 years or earlier, the Life Line at that same age must be checked, and a **star on the Upper Mars mount, especially in a female hand**, can independently indicate a teenage relationship; this combination strengthens further with an influence line running from the Moon area to the Life Line, or with visible disturbance in the Life Line and Heart Line near their start.

The **quality of the line's darkness** carries its own reading, independent of timing:

| Line quality | Reading |
|---|---|
| **Not visible / very faint** | Not a good sign — not "no marriage," but a lack of full commitment or emotional connection to the partner |
| **Very dark / heavy** | Suggests old-fashioned thinking about marriage, a tendency toward rigidity and control; shows pressure on married life, with energy concentrated there because of some ongoing family problem |
| **Normal** (neither too dark nor too light) | Indicates a good married life |
| **Light** | Also a poor sign |

Hand type reshapes this baseline: a fiery hand leans toward independence; an airy hand toward doubt and suspicion (śakkī); an earthy hand toward a long-term, settled kind of relationship. Before predicting marriage, career, or health from any single line, the standing rule is to **read the client's nature first** — many predictions shift once the underlying temperament is known. For a very dark marriage line specifically, check whether a **parasite line** (a thin line running alongside the Life Line in this same area) is also present — this can mean someone unwell is living in the household, placing strain on the marriage from outside the couple itself.

## 3. States of Development: Forms and Combinations

The marriage line's shape and markings carry the bulk of its predictive detail. These fall naturally into four groups — favourable forms, troubled-but-recoverable forms, terminal forms (divorce/widowhood/death), and positional or marked defects.

| Form | Reading | Key qualifier |
|---|---|---|
| Gentle **upward turn** or upward branches (no hook) | Good, happy married life | For a female, also check the Jupiter mount and Mars; for a male, the Venus mount |
| Line continues after a vertical cut, in a smooth/normal quality | Trouble was sorted out; married life continues well | A very dark continuation means the trouble is unresolved and increasing |
| **Triangle** on the line | Very good (rare) | — |
| Line reaches the **Sun mount** without cutting across it | Name, fame, and prosperity after marriage | Rare; if it *cuts* the Sun mount/line instead, it indicates trouble caused by a woman |
| **Square** formed among the lines around the marriage line | Protection — a worsening married life recovers or steadies | A gap and up/down lines nearby still show the disturbance happened, even though the square protects against total breakdown |
| Short **downward branches** | Normal ups and downs, found in over half of all hands | Only becomes a concern if the branches grow long enough to cut the Heart Line |

| Troubled form | Reading | Key qualifier |
|---|---|---|
| **Cut by a vertical line**, then continuing | Sudden break in an otherwise good married life; the break's age is read from the Life Line, not here | Can indicate the sudden or accidental death of the spouse |
| Line **cut repeatedly** | Alternating good and bad phases throughout married life | — |
| **Downward** toward the Heart Line (touching or cutting it) | Dull, low-connection married life, mental strain, heartbreak | Cutting the Heart Line outright is the worst version — the end of married life will be sorrowful |
| **Zigzag** shape (rare) | Alternating good–bad phases | — |
| A line **crosses/cuts** the marriage line from elsewhere | Third-party influence, or the person's own disturbed mental state, troubling the marriage | Must be distinguished from the Mercury line (see §4) |
| **Island** on the line | Position tells the timing: at the start = trouble before/during the engagement; in the middle = trouble in mid-married-life; at the end = trouble in the last phase | A **small** island is more dangerous than a large one — the person feels the trouble more acutely, having grown used to the partner |
| **Fork** at the start or end | Neither is good: at the start it shows distraction/an initial-level problem; at the end, lost interest in the marriage | A fork from the *middle*, one branch up and one down, is read as ordinary upward/downward branches instead |
| **Mole (til)** on the line or on the Mercury mount | Never a good sign; on the line itself, can form a widowhood yoga | — |
| **Cross, star**, on a drooping or straight line | Spoils the line regardless of which | — |
| Extra parallel lines **below** the marriage line | Relationships before marriage | — |
| Extra parallel lines **above** the marriage line | Relationships/enjoyment continuing after marriage | If on **both sides**, both before and after |

| Terminal/major form | Reading | Key qualifier |
|---|---|---|
| Line **hooks upward** (small to long) | No marriage, or marital trouble if it does occur; books say "lifelong bachelor" | A gentle upward turn without a hook is the opposite, favourable sign — the two must not be confused |
| Line **hooks downward** | A problem that will never be solved | Opposite of the upward hook |
| **Circle** at the end, or a **vertical line cutting** the marriage line | Death of the spouse — sudden/accidental in the vertical-cut form | — |
| Shape **>--<** | Divorce indication (one of several, not the only one) | — |
| Line **bends down and stops on the Venus mount** | Divorce | Distinct from a separate line *starting from* Venus and cutting across the marriage line (§4); shape also matters: a sudden drop = a sudden problem, a gradual curve = a slowly building one; seen in **both hands** suggests the divorce was pre-decided in the person's mindset |
| Line **cuts the Sun line** | Intercaste marriage with some loss of respect (mān-sammān) | Reasoning: cutting the line of honour reduces respect |
| Line extends across the Heart Line **to the Mind Line** | Marriage between partners of different religions; also a divorce combination, trouble typically arising after a child is born (disputes over the child's upbringing) | — |
| A **second line starts above** the marriage line after it ends | Second marriage | The first marriage line still exists; disturbance leads the person to decide on a second union |
| **Fork at the end**, combined with a line from the Venus mount cutting across | Family/household circumstances causing marital trouble | The fork alone is not bad; the cutting line is what is not good |

> [!CAUTION]
> **Caution**: for any downward branch reaching toward the Heart Line, first check whether the cutting line originates from the **Mars mount** or from the marriage line itself. A Mars-line cut is far more dangerous — fights, accidents, and greater trouble — than a branch from the marriage line, though both disturb the mind. In every case, the marriage line should ideally have **no cutting connection to the Heart Line** anywhere along it.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Two lines, not two marriages.** When two lines are present, only the longer and clearer/darker one is read as the true marriage line; the short, faint one is set aside. This can sometimes mean a light relationship or marriage negotiation that broke off before a second, real marriage followed — again judged by deśa-kāla.

## 4. Signs, Markings & Practical Indications

**Distinguishing a genuine cutting line from the Mercury line.** A line crossing the marriage line can show third-party influence or disturbed mental state, but it must not be mistaken for the Mercury line, which naturally runs through this same area and becomes the "healer line." Trace where the cutting line actually begins, and ask about the person's profession — a medical professional, for instance, may show this as a healing line rather than a marital disruption.

![Fig 1. Mercury Line vs. a Line Cutting the Marriage Line](/lecture-notes-images/28/01-me-line-vs-line-cutting-marriage-line.png)

**The line from Venus cutting the marriage line.** A separate line starting near the thumb (Venus mount) that rises to cut across the marriage line is, per the books, read as the native potentially killing their own life partner — or the partner being violent toward them. This must be confirmed through practice and observation, never forced or assumed from a drawn line, and it applies equally to male and female hands.

> [!CASE]
> **Case Example**: consider a news case of a woman convicted of killing her husband. Such a line should never be read in isolation — it must be checked alongside the Mars mount, the thumb, and specifically the "murderer's thumb" (with its pent-up energy, discussed earlier), before any such conclusion can be drawn.

**Yogas for a good married life.** Beyond the line itself, here is a working checklist for a favourable marriage: a good **Jupiter mount** first (a cross on Jupiter, especially in a female hand, improves recovery chances even when the marriage line itself looks poor), then a good **Venus mount** (weighted more heavily in the male hand, since in a female Venus also reflects family circumstances generally, though Venus becomes important in the female hand again at the time of childbirth), overall **hand quality**, **long, fine, beautiful fingers** — especially the Jupiter finger — and a good **fate line in the male hand**, since a lack of career is itself a frequent cause of marital dispute. These combine into the **Satī Yoga**: a good Jupiter mount, long and beautiful fingers, a soft hand, no crooked fingers (especially Jupiter's), and a good Venus mount, taken together.

**Love marriage versus arranged marriage.** In short: if it is not love, it will be arranged — any one of the following combinations may be cited.

| Indicator | Reading |
|---|---|
| Fiery or watery hand | More chances of love marriage (earthy/airy hands can also have one, just less often) |
| Heart Line ends below the Saturn mount | Fewer chances of love marriage; low inclination, more practicality |
| **Ring of Saturn** present | Very serious, practical, inattentive to romance; sometimes read as a "no marriage" sign |
| Heart Line goes straight to the Jupiter mount | Some practicality remains, but love marriage is not ruled out |
| Heart Line curves slightly toward Jupiter, ending near the edge of the palm | "Blind in love," emotional, yet still weighing long-term suitability |
| Cross or Svastika on Jupiter | Not itself an indication of love marriage, but if one happens, it will be a good one, and the chances of a relationship converting into marriage rise |
| Inner/lower Mars good (female hand) | Supports a love-marriage inclination; **outer Mars** specifically for a rebellious hand (one that left home for love) |
| Large gap between the Life Line and Mind Line | Open-mindedness — check together with thumb quality, Sun finger, and Mars mount |

**Disturbed married life.** A checklist of warning signs, each read with the rest of the hand: a **mole on the Venus mount** (bad in all cases, male or female); a **grille** on Venus (at least three vertical and three horizontal lines of equal darkness, read as anxiety over relationships and family); lines on the **second phalange of the thumb** (the more lines, the more marital trouble); a **family ring** without a chain (poor emotional connection — chain present is fine); a **grille, cut, or mole on the Jupiter mount** (a cross there, however, remains fine); and a **crooked Jupiter finger**, read as direct trouble in married life, corresponding astrologically to an afflicted Jupiter. Note that **any blackness or discoloration anywhere on the hand marks that area as troubled** — these fine details must be actively looked for, not assumed away.

> [!CLASS]
> **Cross-reference — the family ring and child lines.** Marriage is also read through the **family ring** (kuṭumba valaya) at the base of the thumb and through fine upward lines appearing directly above the marriage line itself — the earliest indication of a couple's capacity (yogyatā) for children, not their eventual number. These belong properly to the *Children Lines* chapter, but they should always be checked alongside the marriage line when a client's question touches both topics.

![Fig 1. The Family Ring](/lecture-notes-images/30/01-example.png)

> [!CASE]
> **Case Example — Anuradha ji's hand.** A prominent Jupiter mount showed multiple fine lines forming what first looked like two diamonds but, on closer inspection, was identified as a genuine **Teacher's Square** — notably, Anuradha ji works as a teacher, which fits the combination's name. A triangle above and below the square, both favourable, increased Jupiter's tendencies further, and the density of lines on Jupiter suggested a spiritual inclination. The faint curved impression nearby gave only a slight suggestion of a Ring of Solomon — not a true one, since it lacked the full curve — but Anuradha ji confirmed an unusual rapport with birds (mimicking a koel's call and recognising its replies) consistent with that symbol's meaning.

![Fig 2. Hand Example — Anuradha Ji](/lecture-notes-images/28/02-anuradha-ji.png)

> [!CASE]
> **Case Example — Anuradha ji's Life Line.** Two islands on the Life Line corresponded exactly to two real periods of ill health: childhood asthma, cured around age 14–15, and a long stretch of heart surgeries from roughly 32 to 40 following delivery — illustrating how precisely island timing can map onto a person's actual medical history.

> [!CASE]
> **Case Example — Meenu ma'am's hand.** An island on the Mercury line appeared during an illness and disappeared on full recovery. Her marriage line sat a little below the midpoint of the marriage-age area, matching the age at which she was actually married.

> [!CASE]
> **Case Example — partnership-business client.** A client considering a business partnership showed a weak, low-set Mercury finger (reaching only the second phalange of the Sun finger), a thumb bent further backward than usual (lower willpower), and a dark, unsmooth Life Line with a cut to both Life and fate lines around age 30. The reading given was that the chance of a successful partnership was low but not ruled out, pending exact age calculation — illustrating the standing rule to never predict from one feature alone, and to gather the client's actual circumstances (role, investment split, relationship to the partner) before finalising a reading.

**Downward branches, precisely measured.** A final refinement on the troubled-forms table above: the *distance* between a downward branch and the main marriage line changes its severity.

![Fig 1. Downward Branches from the Marriage Line](/lecture-notes-images/29/01-downward-lines.png)

| Downward branch | Reading |
|---|---|
| **Wide** gap between branch and main line | Can lead to divorce |
| **Faint and very close** to the main line | Situation will be bad, but divorce is not a necessary outcome |

## 5. Chapter Summary & Comparison Table

| Reading axis | What to check | What it tells you |
|---|---|---|
| **Presence/absence** | Is a line visible at all on the Mercury mount? | Absence ≠ no marriage; it suggests lack of full commitment, not literal bachelorhood |
| **Darkness/quality** | Very dark, very light, or normal? | Normal = good married life; both extremes are poor signs, for different reasons (rigidity/pressure vs. weak connection) |
| **Direction of the end** | Hooks up, hooks down, turns up gently, bends to Venus? | Gentle upward = happy marriage; hook up = no marriage; hook down = unsolvable problem; bend to Venus = divorce |
| **Marks along the line** | Island, cross, star, mole, fork, cutting line? | Each has a specific, timed meaning (see §3 tables); position along the age scale pinpoints *when* |
| **Relationship to other lines** | Cuts Heart Line? Cuts Sun line? Reaches Mind Line? Reaches Sun mount? | Determines severity of heartbreak, intercaste marriage, inter-religious marriage/divorce, or post-marriage prosperity |
| **Supporting mounts** | Jupiter, Venus, Mars, fate line, family ring | Confirms or offsets what the line alone suggests — the line is never read in isolation |

## 6. Sanskrit Terms Introduced

- **Vivāha Rekhā**: the marriage line, on the Mercury mount.
- **Śīla-Guṇa Rekhā**: an alternate name for the marriage line, emphasising character and quality of bonding over the literal institution of marriage.
- **Deśa-Kāla-Paristhiti**: place, time, and circumstance — the interpretive lens required before reading marriage, career, or health from any line.
- **Saṃdhi Rekhā**: the joint crease of a finger phalange; here, the upper boundary of the marriage-line age scale.
- **Satī Yoga**: the combination of a good Jupiter mount, long fine fingers, a soft hand, straight fingers (especially Jupiter's), and a good Venus mount, associated with a favourable married life.
- **Kuṭumba Valaya**: the family ring at the base of the thumb, cross-referenced here and detailed fully in the Children Lines chapter.
- **Yuti**: conjunction; the principle of reading a finger or mount tilt as if two planets were conjoined, combining both their significations.

## 7. Diagnostic Self-Check

1. Is the marriage line present at all, and if so, is it the longest/darkest of several lines — and have I ruled out mistaking the Mercury line for a cutting line before reading any disturbance?
2. Where exactly does the line end or get marked — a gentle upward turn, a hook, a bend toward Venus, a cut to the Heart Line or Sun line — and does that end-point match a specific, timed event on the age scale?
3. Have I checked the Jupiter mount, Venus mount, fate line, and family ring together with this line, rather than predicting marriage, divorce, or widowhood from the marriage line alone?

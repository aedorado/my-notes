# The Seven Dorsal Hand Archetypes
### Pṛṣṭha Hasta Bheda · Seven Classical Morphologies of the Back Palm

Where the front palm yields four elemental archetypes, the back of the hand carries an entirely separate classification, attributed to Cheiro and known as the Western division. Some texts describe this system with eight types, others with eleven; the practical teaching set out here narrows it to **seven** types — sufficient on its own to derive reliable results.

:::glance
::::left
### IN ONE MINUTE
◆ **Nails carry major weight here**: in back-palm reading, nail shape and skin texture (soft vs. hard) can shift the reading of two otherwise similar-looking hands substantially.
◆ **No single type is a final verdict**: hand type is the "soil" the lines grow in — it must still be combined with every other factor (lines, mounts, finger proportions) before any conclusion is drawn.
◆ **Spatulate and Square are the two best categories**: both combine physical capability with intellectual or transactional strength, without the Elementary hand's lack of forward-thinking ambition.
◆ **Determination improves and softens down the scale**: line quality and refinement generally improve moving from Elementary → Spatulate → Square → Conical → Philosophical → Psychic, though a soft hand type with poor line quality still carries real struggle.

## 1. Foundational Principles & Reading Approach

Viewed from the back for the first time, a hand is generally harder to interpret at a glance than the same hand viewed from the front. **Nails** play a major role in this system — their shape varies considerably (wide and short, long and narrow, or wide, large, and spread out), and this variation, combined with skin texture (soft vs. hard, as established in Chapter A2), can change the reading result even between hands that otherwise look alike.

> [!CAUTION]
> **No Reading Stands Alone**: No single hand-type classification should be treated as a final, standalone conclusion. Every other factor — lines, mounts, finger proportions, skin, and nails — must still be examined together before an accurate result can be given. Hand type describes the "soil" in which the lines (the "seed") will grow — whether that ground is thick, stony, well-irrigated, or fertile — not the final outcome itself.

## 2. The Seven Back-Palm Types at a Glance

1. **Elementary hand** (श्रमिक हाथ / प्रारंभिक हाथ)
2. **Spatulate hand** (चमचाकार हाथ)
3. **Square hand** (वर्गाकार हाथ)
4. **Conical hand**
5. **Philosophical / Naughty hand**
6. **Psychic hand**
7. **Mixed hand** (मिश्रित हाथ)

## 3. Type 1 — Elementary Hand (श्रमिक हाथ / प्रारंभिक हाथ)

### Physical Description

Compared to the other six types, this hand appears thicker and heavier. Viewed from the back, the palm portion looks prominent while the fingers look comparatively less prominent, giving the impression that the palm "takes up more space." The palm shape itself is square rather than rectangular, an appearance influenced by the Mars mount area and a specific part of the palm's base.

Skin is generally hard and coarse, with two sub-variants — a softer version and a truly hard version; the authentic elementary/labourer hand carries the hard-skin variant, without the softness that comes from regular care. The overall form is square and stiff, resisting easy movement (much as a bangle slides easily onto some hands and with difficulty onto others). The wrist is wider and thicker than a typical thin wrist, the thumb is thick in overall proportion, and the nails are generally small.

> [!CAUTION]
> **Small Nails Alone Are Not Sufficient**: Small nails can appear in other hand types too, and do not by themselves confirm an Elementary hand. They are only a meaningful indicator when they coincide with the type's other features — hard skin, a thick wrist, a square and stiff overall shape, and a thick thumb.

All features — color, shape, nail size — are read **in proportion to the individual's own body**, never in isolation; a thicker hand should still be judged relative to the person's own build.

### Mentality and Character

The Elementary hand is generally associated with a "lower" category of mentality or thinking — a description of mindset, not of socioeconomic class. It is **not** required that this hand only appear among the poor or among laborers; it can equally be found among affluent people, where it still reflects the same underlying mindset. It is, however, more commonly seen among those doing greater physical labor and less common among the prosperous.

> [!CAUTION]
> **Hand Type Is the Soil, Not the Whole Prediction**: Whether a person actually does more physical labor cannot be read from hand type alone — the Mercury line, the Jupiter mount, and other lines must also be checked.

Its core mentality can be described, in a simple sense, as work-eat-sleep, with little concern for tomorrow or for the future of children or later generations — even when a real, usable skill is present, there may be no interest in advancing or growing it, and even direct advice or investment opportunity may be met with no interest, which in turn increases life's struggle. Appreciation runs low in this type as well: a child's drawing brought home for praise may be met with a flat "okay, fine," before returning to routine — a pattern distinct from ordinary practicality, since even practical people typically still show attachment to the future. This hand type is not exclusive to men; when found in women, emotional sensitivity may likewise appear comparatively lower than typical.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Hand Quality Shapes the Level of Struggle**: Even where the lines themselves are good, a harder, coarser hand type means results arrive only after considerably more effort and hardship than for a softer-handed counterpart with similar lines.

## 4. Type 2 — Spatulate Hand (चमचाकार हाथ)

### Physical Description

Named for its resemblance to a large, curved ladle, the Spatulate hand looks visually similar to the Elementary hand at first glance, differing mainly in degree:

| Feature | Elementary Hand | Spatulate Hand |
|---|---|---|
| Wrist | Thick/heavy | Thinner |
| Skin | Very hard | Less hard (softer) |
| Nails | Small | Not as small |

### Character and Abilities

Together with the Square hand, the Spatulate hand is considered one of the **two best categories**. Where the Elementary hand relies on physical labor without needing intellectual capacity, the Spatulate-hand person can do both physical and mental/intellectual work — and, crucially, recognizes their own skill and understands how to refine and grow it. Among laborers, for instance, a worker with fine, intricate craft skill who actively thinks about improving that skill — rather than simply repeating it — typically shows this hand type.

Spatulate-hand natives continuously learn new things, never settling for "I've conquered the world" after one skill, and understand how to make money from money. Their underlying philosophy is **learn first, then earn** — distinct from the Square hand, whose natives can potentially earn without first needing to learn a skill. This type can appear in any social class; among laborers, it marks the most skilled and refined craftspeople.

Personality-wise, they are practical and hardworking but highly value independence and cannot be "tied down." They are punctual and disciplined — ending work precisely at the stated hour rather than lingering — a discipline connected to the thumb (the seat of willpower), since setting a personal goal reflects willpower, which in turn builds discipline. They tend to be somewhat talkative, strong followers of customs and fixed obligations (leaving promptly for a set prayer time, for example, then returning to precise, careful work), and have a good understanding of profit and loss, often knowledgeable across many subjects.

## 5. Type 3 — Square Hand (वर्गाकार हाथ)

### Two Ways to Read "Square"

The square classification can apply in two ways: either the palm alone is square-shaped, or the entire hand — palm and fingers together — gives an overall square "look" as a whole, even where strict measurement might technically classify it as rectangular; the overall visual impression is what determines the category here. In some cases the nails may also appear squarish, though this is a subjective, non-universal detail — long fingers, for instance, can still carry very short nails.

> [!TIP]
> **Small Nails — A Dual Effect**: Short or small nails can make a person more investigative by nature — a quality that can be favorable or unfavorable depending on the rest of the hand.

### Character — "Business Hand"

This type is called a "business hand," though this does not mean its natives pursue only business — it refers instead to a strong understanding of transactions: awareness of earnings, expenses, budgeting, and money management. The key distinction from the Spatulate hand is that a Square-hand person can potentially earn without first needing to learn a skill — an innate, born-in ability — where the Spatulate hand must learn before it can earn.

Square-hand natives carry a balanced personality combining emotion and practicality, are strong long-term planners with natural business-minded awareness of their own strengths and weaknesses, and can be strongly determined and resolute.

> [!TIP]
> **Stubbornness vs. Determination Across the Scale**:

| Hand Type | Stubbornness (Jiddī) Level | Determination Level |
|---|---|---|
| Elementary hand | Highest | — |
| Spatulate hand | Lower than Elementary | — |
| Square hand | Lowest of the three | Highest determination (dṛḍha-niścaya) |

> [!CAUTION]
> Being determined and resolute does not mean being stubborn in every instance — the two qualities are related but distinct. Determination level is also shaped further by the thumb and by the inclination of the fingers.

Square-hand natives tend to follow tradition and work toward social good — through wealth where prosperous, through new ideas, or through free service of whatever kind they can offer. They display good etiquette in how they speak, sit, and present themselves, and manage people gracefully; unlike the occasional ego seen in the Elementary hand (for example, resenting a simple act of service), neither the Square nor the Spatulate hand shows this trait. A slightly researcher-minded quality often appears here too, especially where short nails are also present.

Both the Spatulate hand and the Square hand belong to the best overall hand category.

## 6. Type 4 — Conical Hand

### Physical Description

The overall hand appears long and thin, with fingers that narrow or taper forward — a quality best judged from the fingers themselves rather than from palm shape alone. The hand tends to be soft and generally thin, with the middle (Saturn) finger sometimes noticeably long, and nails that are typically beautiful and well-shaped — the kind that looks attractive with nail paint applied.

### Character

A fondness for luxury and comfort marks this type — not merely a desire for it (which almost everyone shares to some degree) but an actual capacity to experience and feel comfort in daily life, with the precise amount received depending on the quality of the lines.

Conical-hand natives tend to be **moody**, since soft-handed people generally show fluctuating emotion, paired with lower patience that can tip into impulsiveness over small frustrations. They are highly creative, not only in drawing or writing but also in clever, skillful speech — getting a point across cleverly, without the listener fully grasping what was said, while still achieving the intended goal, is itself a form of this creativity, alongside a facility for multitasking several things at once. A fondness for food is common, often paired with a genuine enjoyment of cooking and hosting guests generously.

This type can also show some **difficulty settling** into new places — a more persistent unease than the ordinary discomfort anyone might feel sleeping away from familiar surroundings, which worsens where the person's nature is already soft, moody, impulsive, or stubborn.

> [!TIP]
> **Age Is Required for Prediction**: Predictions involving line-based daśā or timing calculations (where a 30-year, 35-year, or 40-year mark falls along a line) cannot be made without knowing the person's actual age.

## 7. Type 5 — Philosophical Hand / "Naughty Hand"

### Physical Description

Visually similar to the Conical hand (both carry thin fingers), the distinguishing feature here is **prominent, raised knuckles** at the finger joints, unlike the smooth, soft appearance of a plain Conical hand. Not every finger need show this prominence — whichever fingers do show it carry the associated planetary quality more strongly than the others.

This type has three recognized sub-categories:
1. **Simple naughty finger** — prominent knuckles, nothing further.
2. **Thin skin with visible veins/nerves** — especially soft, thin skin allowing veins to show from the back of the hand, which slightly modifies the reading.
3. **Crooked/zigzag shape** — the hand or specific fingers appear visibly bent, with the "crookedness" impact centered on whichever finger's own significations are affected.

### Character

This type adds a **philosophical mindset** on top of the Conical hand's baseline qualities, bringing philosophy into nearly every subject discussed — a "darshanik" way of viewing things. It is commonly seen among actors drawn to meaningful, message-driven roles rather than mainstream entertainment, and among stand-up comedians, whose elevated creativity carries a similar philosophical inclination; motivational speaking is another common fit.

Emotionally, this type can be very moody, very impulsive, and quite stubborn — the more crooked the finger and the softer the skin, the more pronounced the moodiness tends to be. A particular fussiness about comfort is common (insisting on a specific plate, or a specific familiar corner of a bed), in clear contrast to the Elementary hand's undemanding ease with whatever is offered.

> [!INFO]
> **Career Fit**: the more conical or philosophical the hand, the more likely a fit with healing professions, medicine, or creative fields. Fingerprint analysis (and modern DMIT-style testing) can further clarify true career fit — many people leave an established career around age 40–45 simply because it was never their true calling, only an income necessity.

> [!NOTE]
> Prominent knuckles in this type tend to accompany a calmer, more secretive personality; a plain Conical hand without prominent knuckles is correspondingly less calm and less secretive.

## 8. Type 6 — Psychic Hand

### Physical Description

An extension of the Conical/Philosophical hand's qualities taken further — even softer and more delicate, comparable to a royal or newborn's hand: extremely soft, almost fragile, giving the impression that one should be careful even touching it.

### Character

The greater the softness, the greater the sensitivity, affecting nature, personality, and even health — this type is more prone to colds, coughs, congestion, and stomach upsets. Much like the touch-me-not plant, whose leaves curl inward at the slightest touch, the smallest lack of praise or appreciation can spoil this type's mood.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Conditional Spiritual Potential**: where prominent knuckles are present alongside this softness, the person may reach very high spiritual levels. Where knuckles are absent, the tendency instead favors staying within a comfort zone, avoiding physical exertion, and preferring that things be brought directly rather than pursued.

Line color tends to appear lighter in this type. Moving across the full scale — Elementary → Spatulate → Square → Conical → Philosophical → Psychic — line quality generally improves as the hand itself softens and refines.

> [!CAUTION]
> A refined, soft hand type does not by itself end a person's struggle: if the line quality itself does not improve (remaining thick or coarse, for instance), life's difficulty persists regardless of how soft or delicate the hand type appears.

Psychic-hand natives often prefer staying within their comfort level, capable of holding the same job for years without wanting change, and benefit from being given a fixed, particular routine; even a small disruption to that routine can provoke a strongly disproportionate reaction, and change of any kind is generally unwelcome.

## 9. Type 7 — Mixed Hand (मिश्रित हाथ)

This category applies when a hand does not clearly fit any of the preceding six types. The reading method is to:

1. Identify which of the six types the hand **most closely resembles** — this becomes the major type.
2. Note any deviations from that major type (for example, a predominantly Square hand with unusually long fingers, or with short nails).
3. Layer the qualities of the secondary, deviating type onto the major type's baseline reading.

Mixed-hand natives tend to multitask well, since they carry the combined qualities of two or three underlying types — practical, emotional, and creative all at once. Career often shows ups and downs, reflecting genuine internal indecision between paths pulled in different directions by the blended qualities, and this type tends to be full of ideas. Predictions for a Mixed hand are most reliably confirmed through detailed study of the **lines**, more so than for any single, clearly defined type.

## 10. A Related but Separate Topic: Fingertip Shape

Finger shape — specifically the shape of the fingertip — is a quality separate from the overall hand type described above, and further modifies any reading drawn from it. Some fingertips are squared, some rounded, and some show a distinct "bounce" or bulge near the top, resembling a spinning top. These individual qualities (hand type, finger shape, nail shape, knuckle prominence) are best studied one at a time before being combined — once combined, the reading becomes considerably more precise.

## 11. Risk-Taking Across the Seven Types

Among all seven types, the **Square hand** shows the strongest risk-taking tendency — not the Elementary hand, which might be assumed from its association with hardship. A proper assessment of risk-taking requires examining the **Sun finger** and the **thumb** together with the hand type. For the specific risk of leaving an already well-established position for something entirely unfamiliar, the thumb is examined first, followed by the Jupiter and Sun fingers, alongside the career lines on the palm (sometimes appearing as two lines, one running straight and another joining from the side) — a topic developed more fully in later study.

## 13. Sanskrit Terms Introduced

- **Pṛṣṭha Hasta**: The back (dorsal) view of the hand, the basis of this seven-type classification.
- **Dṛḍha-niścaya**: Firm determination/resolve — the defining strength of the Square hand, distinct from mere stubbornness.
- **Darshanik**: Philosophical in outlook — the defining quality added by the Philosophical/Naughty hand.

## 14. Diagnostic Self-Check

1. Has the hand been assessed from the back, with nail shape and skin texture weighed alongside overall form, before naming a type?
2. Has this hand-type reading been checked against the lines, mounts, and finger proportions rather than treated as a final conclusion on its own?
3. If the hand does not clearly fit one of the first six types, has the major type been identified first, with the deviating qualities layered on top, rather than defaulting to "Mixed" prematurely?

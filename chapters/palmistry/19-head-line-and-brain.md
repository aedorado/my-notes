# The Head Line: Intellect, Mental Focus & Curvature
### Mastiṣka Rekhā · The Manifestation Tool of the Whole Hand

The Brain Line — mastiṣka rekhā — is the base of the entire hand: however healthy the rest of the body, nothing functions if the brain itself fails, and in the same way, however strong the Life Line and Fate Line may be, a disturbed thought process undermines everything built on top of it. A person whose life and career lines are excellent may still be seen worrying for no visible reason — that unease belongs to the Brain Line, not to circumstance. The reverse also holds: a person with a head-strong, very good Brain Line can bear a difficult Life Line and Fate Line and carry every trouble that comes.

- **Sanskrit Nomenclature**: Mastiṣka Rekhā; also called the "air line," and in various sources mātṛ rekhā, śīrṣa rekhā, śiro rekhā, śikhara rekhā, bhoga rekhā, and mastaka rekhā

## 1. Foundational Principles & Anatomy

The Brain Line shows the **thought process**, and because thought underlies every decision and action, it sets the foundation on which everything else in the hand rests. It can be called the **manifestation tool**: a person may decide firmly, "I must build a good career," and in many hands the career line is visible for a time and then fades into a mere impression rather than a formed line — but as the person reaches that age, *if the thought process behind the decision is strong*, the line begins to actually form. A strong Brain Line is therefore not simply a trait among others; it is the mechanism by which intention becomes visible elsewhere on the hand.

The line carries a long list of names beyond Mastiṣka Rekhā — the **air line** (because it shows the thinking process, wandering and changing as thoughts arise through the day), along with mātṛ rekhā (a line that can also speak of the mother), śīrṣa rekhā, śiro rekhā, śikhara rekhā, bhoga rekhā, and mastaka rekhā, among others.

Its origin is generally taken as the point where the Life Line begins, though this is not universal — the Brain Line can also start separately on the Moon mount or the Mercury mount, or end partway across the palm.

> [!IMPORTANT] The Brain Line is not aged like the other lines
> Unlike the Life Line, Fate Line, and Heart Line, the Brain Line is **not read by age along its own length**. A thought that enters the mind in childhood can persist for a lifetime — consider how something said by a relative in childhood can make a grown adult reluctant to visit that relative's house even decades later, because "they behaved that way with me." The Brain Line therefore reflects an **overall life pattern** (such as a tendency toward depression, or its absence) rather than a single dated event, and timing for its indications must be taken from the other lines.

## 2. Structural Variations: Distance from the Life Line, and Origin on the Mounts

### 2.1 Joined with the Life Line

Where the Brain Line begins joined to the Life Line and runs together with it for some stretch — up to age five, or ten, or longer — the person, for that period, follows the family's words and listens to parental guidance, remaining a follower of tradition and, in childhood, dependent on others since no other option exists. Confidence may run slightly lower in this condition, though it is never judged from this sign alone — the thumb, the Sun finger, and the whole hand must be weighed together. Such a person does not oppose much, though a milder tendency to do so can arise later.

> [!WARNING] Common Mistake
> A joined Brain Line should not be read as "I never obeyed my family." The meaning is that the person **listens to the guidance given**, not that they mechanically obey every instruction — this distinction must be applied with judgement, not read literally.

### 2.2 A Slight Gap from the Life Line

A small gap — one side given to the family, the other to one's own thinking — marks children who form their own opinions and state them, yet still listen and do not cut off the other person's words in open opposition. Such children show genuine interest from an early age when family members sit and discuss matters together: they absorb, they contribute, but they do not take the opposite position merely to oppose.

### 2.3 A Large Gap from the Life Line

Measured at roughly the width of a pen's end, or about one finger's breadth — rarely more than this in practice — a wide gap marks a **risk-taker**, self-willed and inclined to run their own course. This self-willed condition is strengthened further when the thumb is very large, the Mercury finger stands apart from the rest, and the Jupiter finger is **not** tilted toward Saturn, all pointing to a strong will-power area. This independence is increasingly common in children today, who want to state their own plans and ask "why is this happening?" rather than simply follow as earlier generations did.

> [!CAUTION] Exception: wide gap, yet the person still defers
> If a connecting line is seen joining the Life Line and the Brain Line despite the wide gap between them, the person's thoughts may genuinely differ from the family's, but they will **not** cut off the other's words and will still ultimately obey.

> [!CASE]
> **Example**: Genuine independence of this kind can show up early — a child who goes away from home to study, manages their own admission into a new class, and buys their own books and copies without waiting for family to do it. The same contrast appears between two kinds of children: one walks with their mother everywhere, another cycles alone needing no one; one has parents deposit college fees, another insists on depositing the fees themselves. The exact degree of this condition differs for every person according to **deśa-kāla-paristhiti** — "book language" stays the same, but the real detail changes from hand to hand. Observation also suggests, beyond what any book states, that when the Life Line and Brain Line separate in this way, the person is able to live away from home comfortably, such as in a hostel — a pattern confirmed across a number of hands showing this separation.

### 2.4 Origin on the Mounts

The Brain Line generally originates from either the **Jupiter mount** or the **Mars mount** (a start slightly above the usual point is still treated as the Jupiter area), and this origin sets the pattern of the person's thinking: a line beginning from Jupiter gives a guru-like mindset, while one beginning from Mars gives a Mars-like mindset — a pattern that colours the person's basic approach to thought from the outset.

An **island** at the exact point where the Brain Line joins or begins indicates **mental pressure** at that age.

## 3. States of Development: Three Line-Forms

| State | Physical Sign | Character / Psychological Manifestation | Real-World Impact |
|---|---|---|---|
| Long join with the Life Line (dependent) | Runs together with the Life Line for an extended stretch before separating, if at all | Follows family guidance, a follower of tradition, slightly lower confidence (verify against thumb and whole hand) | Decisions remain family-anchored for as long as the lines stay joined |
| Slight gap (balanced) | A small, consistent separation from the Life Line | Forms and voices personal opinions while still listening and not opposing outright | A balanced mind that absorbs family input while developing its own view |
| Wide gap, about a pen's width (independent/overdeveloped) | A visible, roughly finger-width separation from the Life Line | Risk-taker, self-willed, runs their own course — intensified by a large thumb, a separated Mercury finger, and an untilted Jupiter finger | Independent decision-making, able to live away from home; a connecting line between the two lines softens this into continued, if independent-minded, obedience |

### Variations by Course and Shape

Beyond the gap from the Life Line, the Brain Line's own path across the palm carries further distinctions:

| Form | Traits |
|---|---|
| **Straight to Mercury, not touching the Heart Line** (rare, wide gap from Heart Line) | Very logical, highly creative, an out-of-the-box thinker, very intelligent — considered a very good Brain Line, found in few people |
| **Sydney Line** (semi-simian; smaller gap from the Heart Line, still crossing to Mercury) | Stubborn — once an idea is set it cannot be removed; very practical yet can also be deeply emotional in its own way; research-oriented, wants to be shown "something different" |
| **Merged Head-and-Heart Line** ("senior line"; only one line, no third line present) | Very sharp mind, very practical, very stubborn; balances heart and mind together, though balancing it well is this type's central difficulty — if balance fails, irritability and suspicion (śakkī) increase |

> [!CASE]
> **Example**: A hand still from **Umang Taneja**, the nāḍī-astrology presenter known for challenging astrologers, shows a perfectly straight line crossing the palm. Whether it should be classed as the Sydney line or the related "senior" type is a small distinction that can be set aside; the shape connects more usefully to his having built an entirely independent identity and a nāḍī-astrology practice run in his own name.

The merged head-and-heart line is judged first by whether it is **dark or light**, and second by whether it **rises in the middle or slopes downward** toward the Moon before coming back up. A line sloping **downward** shows the person listens more to the **mind**; a line rising **upward** shows the person listens more to the **heart**. A person unable to balance the two — sometimes following the heart, sometimes the mind — becomes irritable and anxious, as intelligent as they are suspicious.

## 4. Signs, Markings & Practical Indications

### 4.1 Where the Brain Line Ends

| End Point | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Mercury mount** | Mind runs toward reading, writing, gardening, employment, research, logic, mathematics, and technology |
| **Sun mount, stopping there** | Wants name and fame; follows rules and a set educational pattern (graduation, post-graduation, course, then job); not extraordinary unless another line or condition (such as a Mercury line) is also present |
| **Sun mount, with the line continuing beyond it** | Uses the mind more deeply and can become genuinely extraordinary; the line should continue below the mount toward Mars |
| **Saturn, or between Saturn and Sun** | Favours rules, regulations, working alongside others, and security — a fixed income, a home, "dal-roti going comfortably," with no appetite for upheaval; thinking becomes reserved, with some fear and Saturnine negativity |
| **Mars** | Very good if the line's overall condition is good — a strong personality; if the condition is poor, the reading is correspondingly poor |
| **Moon (straight, wavy, or sudden)** | See the wavy-line discussion below |

### 4.2 The Wavy Brain Line

A wavy line calls for calming the person first — "why are you so troubled, so confused?" — since such a person cannot stay firm on one statement. The line rising shows happiness and openness to learning something new; the line dipping down shows renewed emotional disturbance, with high mood swings and a lack of mental clarity. Where the line ends matters greatly: a straight run to the Moon mount is rarely seen at all, since it would have to cross the palm's middle; a **sudden** drop to the Moon shows a mind that was logical and practical (Mars-like) abruptly changing character; and a **wavy** line reaching the Moon mount is considered genuinely dangerous — high mood swings, a tendency to live in imagination as a daydreamer (not necessarily a negative trait in itself), the possibility of depression, and thoughts that change as quickly and as often as the Moon itself.

### 4.3 Lines Cutting the Brain Line

A **Mars line** (rising from the Lower Mars area) cutting across the Brain Line is an enemy line, and may indicate depression, anxiety, confusion, lack of mental clarity, headache, or migraine — the feeling that "however much I try to do right, some trouble comes," even where the Life Line itself is good. Where such a cutting line continues on to the **Fate Line**, the career itself may remain good, yet mental trouble persists for some other reason — a child's health issue, or the person's own — distinguishing physical trouble from purely mental trouble.

### 4.4 Toward the Moon: Danger from Water

Where the Brain Line travels to the Moon mount with many small **downward branches**, this indicates danger associated with water — a reading equally valid when the Life Line travels toward the Moon. This can mean an accident, cough-related trouble, anxiety, addiction, or trouble arising from a female, with the related mental disturbance tied to any of these.

### 4.5 The Writer's Fork and the Double Brain Line

A fork splitting the Brain Line into two branches at its end — resembling a "four" — is called the **writer's fork**: one branch travels to Mercury, the other to Mars or the Moon, carrying the combined qualities of both destinations. Such a person can be a good writer or artist, with a mind that runs easily to business and can handle many things at once — a genuine multitasker. The drawback is that a divided line means a mind that is never fully calm, however much multitasking is achieved. A **double Brain Line** shows high intelligence paired with a duality of mind — scattered attention moving between tasks — and trouble intensifies if the person is also more sensitive, or if the line's end takes on a "watery" quality.

### 4.6 The Broken Brain Line and the Scorpion Tail

A **broken** Brain Line shows a lack of stability in thought, or a drastic change of direction — the example given is a deeply addicted person who suddenly abandons everything to become a sādhu. Repeated breaks show repeatedly changing thought patterns, and if influence lines are also seen on the Fate Line, the person took someone as a role model for a time, changed their life to follow it, and later changed again under a different influence; head-related problems and headache are also associated with this break pattern.

A Brain Line that curves at its very end like a **scorpion's tail**, whether short or long, marks a **secretive** personality — one from whom the full matter can never quite be drawn — and a strong, stubborn personality, especially where the straight portion before the curve is long. Such a person struggles to leave old habits and old memories, may collect antiques or old things (sometimes usefully, sometimes to the irritation of others at home), and tends to live somewhat in the past, becoming suspicious; the longer and straighter the line before the tail, the more this tendency troubles those around them, alongside a persistent anxiety.

> [!CLASS]
> **Classical Reference**: Books describe a Brain Line that turns sharply toward Saturn as **Alpāyu** (short life). This remains unconfirmed as a firm rule and should be verified in practice on an actual hand before being treated as definitive.

### 4.7 Islands, Health Markers, and Sensitive Indicators

| Location of Island | Meaning |
|---|---|
| End of the Brain Line, under the Mercury mount | Migraine; memory loss or dementia in later age |
| Brain Line stops at the Sun mount, island there | Sun-related problems: eyes, the nervous system, and heart (cross-check the Heart Line) |
| Brain Line under Saturn, island there | Depression (Saturn already carries a negative, fearful quality) |
| Short Brain Line ending early, island at the end | ENT problem |
| Life Line with an island near Jupiter | ENT problem present since childhood |

> [!CAUTION] Sensitive — Suicidal Tendency Indicators
> These are read together, never from a single sign alone: the Mind Line (or a branch from it) running straight to the Moon and ending with a **cross or star**; a line that suddenly **cuts the Brain Line** and stops it from continuing; and a **mole (til)** at the very end of the line, which may also indicate depression on its own.

> [!WARNING] Blindness Indicator
> A **mole or black-coloured spot on the Mind Line under the Sun mount** is read as indicating **blindness from birth**.

A further health combination, held for the dedicated health teaching, pairs a **Saturn finger tilted toward the Sun** with a **star on the Saturn mount**: this advises a doctor's check-up, since the body carries a tendency toward some health issue — reasoned from the Sun's role in health and Saturn's finger overlaying it. The same logic applies in reverse with a Jupiter–Sun mix-up, where the fingers tilt toward each other; combined with a mark at a specific age, this can indicate when a health issue is likely to surface.

## 5. Two End-of-Line Symbols: Diamond Chain and Canopy

Before turning fully to the Brain Line, two symbols can appear at the end of any line but are most often seen at the end of the Fate Line or the Sun Line, and are worth carrying alongside every other line discussed in this chapter and the next.

> [!INFO] Diamond Chain — the wish-fulfilment sign
> A diamond-shaped chain at the end of a line is the "wish fulfilment" sign (icchā-pūrti kā chain). On the Fate Line, the line rises and forms this diamond shape at its end; on a short Sun Line, the diamond appears at its last point. Its meaning is not that results arrive only at the end of life — rather, it **doubles the strength of the whole line**, so that whatever that line already promises (name, fame, or wealth) is brought to fulfilment throughout.

> [!INFO] Canopy — the protection sign
> A circle at the end of a line, called the **Canopy**, functions like the square already taught: it is a sign of protection. On the Life Line, for instance, however many ups and downs the line shows along its length, a circle at its end means the whole life is protected despite them.

## 6. Two Further Mind Line Combinations

Two further combinations belong equally to the reading of the Mind Line itself.

If the Mind Line reaches the **Moon mount** and is crossed there by horizontal lines, or surrounded by many such lines, this points to **depression or mood swings** — some books describe this outright as *pāgalpana* (madness), though the word can be understood in several ways, and the general principle holds that whichever mount a line travels to, an afflicted mount there brings trouble related to that mount.

Where the Mind Line **touches the Heart Line**, or a branch from the Heart Line joins the Mind Line (touching outright is rare; a branch is the more common form), the person is caught between heart and mind — trying to use feeling and logic together without ever truly merging them, and so remaining perpetually stuck between the two. The direction the branch actually flows in — read by following the line's course closely — tells whether it originates from the Heart Line or the Mind Line.

> [!CASE]
> **Example — a joined start with a youth-triangle**: Consider a Brain Line starting joined to the Life Line, with a small triangle at that point and many fine lines running to the Moon, alongside several Mars lines cutting the Mind Line. Read together: the joined start shows a family-following mind; the triangle shows property built by the parents in the person's youth (in one such case confirmed as roughly age 15–16, when the family house was indeed built); the lines to the Moon show good imagination paired with slight anxiety; and the Mars lines show alternating happiness and disturbance.

> [!CASE]
> **Example — independence abroad, heart over mind**: A Brain Line touching the Life Line before separating and running long, all the way to Mars, reads as a very strong personality — consistent with a woman who built an independent business abroad and settled her husband's entire family at a good level, later facing a divorce she had not herself sought. Her Heart Line leaning toward the Mind Line, rather than the reverse, is specifically **not** a good sign: it shows the heart dominating the mind, a condition that can leave a person confused for many years, and is to be distinguished carefully from the more favourable case of a Mind Line leaning toward the Heart Line.

> [!CASE]
> **Example — a mind that listens to the mother**: A Brain Line joined to the Life Line for a long stretch reads as a mind that listens closely to the mother. In one such case this was confirmed when, asked whether she would work or study after her upcoming marriage, a young woman of about 18–20 immediately agreed to her mother's wish that she not work. A triangle in her hand pointed to property likely to be built after marriage, and a slightly outward-set Mars mount showed a person who never directly opposes or refuses, while still carrying inner command and confidence.

> [!CASE]
> **Example — the writer's fork in practice**: A Brain Line running straight to Mars with a fork at the end, alongside an island on the line itself, marks a multitasker — fitting for a writer of poetry and articles — while a line from the Heart Line leaning toward the Brain Line shows a person who thinks practically but cannot act without the heart's involvement, so that the heart ultimately dominates even where practicality is the stated intention.

## 7. A Technical Note: Chain vs. Parasite Line

Two close-running markings are easily confused and are worth distinguishing before reading any line's signs in detail. A **chain (jañjīra)** is a line with chain-like, clustered links, seen most often on the family ring below the thumb and at the very start of lines — such a cluster should be read as a chain, not as a series of separate islands. A **parasite line** instead runs very close to, and touching, a main line (such as the Fate Line) along its length, sometimes barely visible for how closely it sits — likened to a termite that lives inside the house and causes trouble from within. The distinguishing rule is simple: a line that **cuts across** another forms a chain; a line that runs alongside, uncut and attached, is a parasite line.

## 9. Diagnostic Self-Check

1. How far does this Brain Line sit from the Life Line, and does a connecting line between them soften an otherwise wide, independent gap?
2. Where does the Brain Line actually end — Mercury, Sun, Saturn, Mars, or the Moon — and does it stop there or continue beyond?
3. Is the line's course toward the Moon straight, sudden, or wavy, and what does that distinction change in the reading?
4. Are any Mars (enemy) lines cutting the Brain Line, and do they continue on to affect the Fate Line as well?
5. Is there a fork, a break, or a scorpion-tail curve at the line's end, and which of the three readings — multitasking, instability, or secretiveness — fits what else the hand shows?
6. Since this line is not aged by its own length, which other line (Life, Fate, or Heart) should be used to time the event in question?
7. Are there sensitive indicators present — a cross or star where the line reaches the Moon, a sudden cut, or a mole at its end — that call for the full combination to be checked before anything is said aloud?

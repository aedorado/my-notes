### B1. Nidhi – drishti and lordship
> [!FAQ] If a dṛṣṭi (aspect) activates a bhāva and the planet sitting in it, will the houses that planet **owns** also be affected?

1. `[Teacher]` Answered "No" first, then gave the analogy: if someone breaks my leg, how much can I look after my home? The lord of a house is affected, so the effect **does go** to the house he owns. If the "owner of a house" gets an aśubha dṛṣṭi, aśubha kartarī (hemming), or war (graha-yuddha) with aśubha grahas, the owner is afflicted and cannot look after the house; "some problem will come."
2. `[Student Nidhi]` ➖ concluded that whatever a planet is kāraka of will also be affected. Teacher: "Hmm, look, the attempt is to explain" (no confirmation).
3. `[Teacher]` Reasoning: a graha is able to give results by its **sthiti (position) and prabhāva (influence)**. It will give results of (a) where it sits, (b) where it aspects, (c) what it owns, so **all the results reduce proportionally**. He praised Nidhi's logical grasp.

## Segment F
*Lecture: how to read results, and sāmarthya*

### F1. Sun and Saturn together in Leo lagna (worked example) `[Teacher]`
- **Lagna** gives independent behaviour, body structure, complexion, height, weight, and wealth (health is wealth).
- Leo lagna → sthira (steady) nature. Sun in Leo: **agni tattva**; Leo is a krūra, viṣama, fire sign; Mars is fire; so the native is very angry. Saturn joined converts the anger into **irritability** (chiḍ-chiḍ), no open anger; abuse will still come.
- **Saturn's effect on the Sun:** the Sun is honest and disciplined; Saturn is not. The native wants to be disciplined but cannot hold it; wants to be honest but honesty does not last.

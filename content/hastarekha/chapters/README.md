# Chapters Directory

This folder contains the authored textbook chapters for the **Hasta Sāmudrika Śāstra** online reader.

The reader is strictly dedicated to compiled textbook chapters. Raw lecture notes and flashcards are maintained separately in the Study Guide (`/study`). When a chapter is in editorial preparation, the reader displays a clean in-progress card directing students to the lecture archives in the study guide.

For the full 27-chapter master mapping matrix and authoring instructions, see:
👉 [**CHAPTER_MAPPING.md**](../CHAPTER_MAPPING.md)

### Frontmatter Standard:
Every chapter in this directory must define its authoritative `chapter_code`:
```yaml
---
id: "ch-10-mount-of-jupiter"
part: "PART D · THE MOUNTS"
chapter_code: "D1"
title: "The Mount of Jupiter: Seat of Dharma & Dignity"
subtitle_sanskrit: "Guru Parvata · Leadership, Teacher's Square & Apex"
essence: "The Jupiter mount is the seat of knowledge, discernment, and leadership..."
read_time: "14 min read"
---
```

- `03-dorsal-archetypes.md`

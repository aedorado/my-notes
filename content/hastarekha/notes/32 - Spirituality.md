# Palmistry Class — Adhyātma (Spirituality) in the Hand

> [!NOTE] Scope of this class
> A "small chapter" on adhyātma (Spirituality). Nothing is new: the teacher says everything has already been taught, and today it is **combined from all sides** into one place. Sequence of the class: (1) perspective → (2) hand types applied to spirituality → (3) planets/indicators to check → (4) Jupiter finger, Jupiter mount, other indicators → (5) professional/spiritual-career indications → (6) hand quality, colour, line density → (7) live case hands → (8) student's own hand and questions.

## 1. Perspective: what "spirituality" means to different people

> [!IMPORTANT] Core principle
> Before reading spirituality in a hand, one needs a **perspective**. If four or five people in the class are asked, each gives a different opinion; each has a different way of understanding adhyātma. This can be understood from the client's **deśa-kāla-paristhiti** (place, time, circumstances) and from the client's **nature**.

| Perspective | Description (as taught) |
|---|---|
| **Religious / devotional** | Totally absorbed in īśvara bhakti; nothing else is visible. Does a lot of meditation/sādhanā, reads scriptures, feels "this is how I follow spirituality". |
| **Practical / service-based** | May not do pūjā-pāṭh or ring temple bells, but serves others, feeds others, stays connected with nature. "That is my spirituality." |
| **Seeker / "who am I"** | Like when **ketu influence** increases: the person starts thinking "Who am I? Where did I come from?" and goes in search of it. |
| **Psychological / peace of mind** | Person is very troubled and turns to spirituality for mental peace; can be understood psychologically. |
| **Event-triggered** | Life was going well, then some event occurs and one is inclined towards spirituality. Could be a **depression phase**, or a problem with someone close, after which one takes more refuge in God. |

> [!NOTE]
> Which one applies "depends on our thinking — how we take it". All these things have to be understood first.

> [!WARNING] Practical note on questions
> Clients rarely ask spirituality questions. Career comes up most; married life and children also; spirituality questions are fewer, and not guaranteed to come.

## 2. Step one: quality of the hand (hand types applied to spirituality)

The first thing to look at is the **hand quality** (type of hand). Hand types were already studied; now each is connected to spirituality. The teacher notes this is only a general, "rough" overview; the individual hand will tell the actual result. Starting with the fiery hand (most hands in the class are fiery).

### 2.1 Fiery hand

- Teacher's prompt: "If someone has a fiery hand, what is the likelihood?"
- **Student (Harishchandra) answer:** such a person would not be one to do only daily pūjā/karmakāṇḍa; not a *virakti* (renunciate) type.
- **Teacher's confirmation/reasoning:** fiery hand wants **action** in everything; fiery meant (from the start) fun, **thrill, adventure**; wants some activity.
- Likely spiritual expressions:
  - Fond of **yātrā (pilgrimages/travel)**.
  - Could take up **yogāsana / yoga** and link it to spirituality.
  - Could start a **spiritual mission**; will gather people, will create some purpose/objective.
  - **Mantra jāpa**, because it is a process with activity.

> [!NOTE] Prompting method
> Teacher asked students to think "according to today's environment" what a fiery person would do, and drew the answers out.

### 2.2 Watery hand

- Qualities recalled: **emotions, imagination (kalpanā), intuition**.
- If a watery person does mantra jāpa: sits and **gets deeply absorbed**.
- Emotion-based path: **prema mārga** (path of love); e.g. like **Mīrā** — once someone is accepted, they are accepted with pure emotion.
- Intuition can be very sharp.
- Interest in **art** → **kīrtana, bhajana**.

### 2.3 Earthy hand

- More **practical**, more **karma-pradhāna**.
- **Student answer:** may follow the ancestral/traditional pūjā-pāṭh practices passed down by forefathers.
- Teacher added: work comes first; **no show-off pūjā**; "**karma itself is his pūjā**".
- Can become a **nature-lover** ("grounded person").
- Takes everything practically; looks after **basic needs of everyone**.

### 2.4 Airy hand

- Student: bhakti rules keep **changing**; cannot stay in one place. Teacher: also goes towards **research/search**.
- Brings **thought (vichāra)** — desire and thought.
- Likely expressions:
  - **Research mode**; searches for answers: "Who am I? What is ātmā? Where did I come from? Where did creation originate?"
  - Reads **many books**; may go to a **guru**.
  - **Tarka-vitarka**; may go into **debate**.
  - Also tends to **spread (prasāra) his bhakti** — student example: ISKCON. Teacher agreed it can happen; added that **fire** is also present a bit there, "but that's different for everyone; if we assume it so".

> [!SUMMARY] Hand type → spiritual tendency
> | Hand type | Likely spiritual style |
> |---|---|
> | Fiery | Action, travel/yātrā, yoga, spiritual mission, mantra jāpa |
> | Watery | Deep absorption, prema/bhakti mārga (Mīrā), kīrtana/bhajana, intuition |
> | Earthy | Traditional family practice, karma-as-pūjā, nature, practical service |
> | Airy | Research, questioning (who am I), books, guru, debate, spreading bhakti |

## 4. Jupiter and other spiritual indicators

### 4.1 Finger phalanges (parva)

> [!TIP] First phalanx
> Look at the **first phalanx of all fingers** for spirituality. The first phalanx indicates **adhyātma**.

- Should be slightly **bigger than the other two** phalanges (slightly more space).
- Not necessary that it be the same in all hands; if not, no problem.
- **Clean**, **not too many lines**, and slightly **puffed up (phulā huā)**.
- **Puffed = active.** Comparison: the upper part of the **thumb** (willpower) — we see its activeness; same with the **fingertips**.
- From here fingerprints will be discussed later (teacher estimates ~2–3 classes later); after that, career (fingerprints play a big role in deciding career).

> [!IMPORTANT] Conical fingers
> **Conical finger** (thin, cone-like at the top) is also considered a **spiritual finger**.

> [!IMPORTANT] Knotty fingers (seen from the back of the hand)
> Knots in fingers = **philosophical hand**: more of a **seeker (khojī) tendency**.
> - Nails of seeker type: **short nails** (student recalled; teacher confirmed "short nails are the seeker nails").
> - Their approach tends to be **philosophical / searching**.

### 4.2 Jupiter mount

What to check, in order:
1. **Area** of the mount (how large).
2. **How raised** it is.
3. **How clean** it is.

| Marking on Jupiter mount | Interpretation |
|---|---|
| **Teacher square** | Can be a **preacher**; can give dharma-jñāna; **motivational speaker**. |
| **Ring of Solomon** | Very sharp **intuition**; **sixth sense**; **psychic ability**; (named after a king who could understand the language of birds). |
| **Diagonal lines** (two lines going diagonally across mount, either slant) = **line of sympathy** | **Compassion towards others**. |

> [!EXAMPLE] Linking markings to the spiritual styles (from §1)
> - **Service/nature oriented spirituality** (serving others, contributing to nature) → **diagonal line (sympathy)** makes it better.
> - Wanting to **spread knowledge** (pracāra-prasāra) → **teacher square** is good.
> - **Intuition-based work** → **Ring of Solomon** is necessary.

> [!NOTE] Ring of Solomon — two forms
> Two ways it can appear (shown by the teacher with drawings): a **semicircle** arc; either form of semi-circular shape counts. Also, as with the rings taught earlier, lines below the finger (“the lines we studied under all the rings”) can form it. The teacher noted one student hand where it was visible.

> [!NOTE]
> "Generally on the Jupiter mount all the symbols we studied are good." So Jupiter mount is looked at first.

### 4.3 Line of intuition

- Line that emerges from the **Chandra (Moon) mount**, going across (shown by gesture).
- Generally it is **not formed fully**; in some hands it is short — treat even the short one as line of intuition.
- Sometimes only an **impression**: begins here, begins there, missing in the middle → "not yet fully formed".

> [!IMPORTANT] Counting rule
> "If any **two or more — meaning three — points** match, we can say that the person may go more towards spirituality." *(Wording as spoken: "do ya do se jyada matlab teen point".)*

### 4.4 Mind line (headline)

- **Head line sloping towards the Moon mount** → Moon = **mind** (manas); person is moved by **emotions**. Even if the hand is airy, a touch of emotion will come in.
- Teacher asked about a PDF picture of **loops** — loops are on the Moon mount; the teacher will teach loops later ("a loop on the Chandra mount that we connect with spirituality"). PDF with loops not yet available; teacher will compile and send. *(See Logistics below.)*

### 4.5 Mystic cross

- Cross between the **heart line** and **head line** (seen in the first class). Lines must **not** be "passing through" — i.e. **an individual cross** between them, not the one made by a fate line crossing and a line coming from the heart line downward (that is not mystic cross).
- Very **rare** in hands.

### 4.6 Moon mount

- Should be **slightly raised** (like Mars mount, which sticks outward).
- **Clean**, without too many cuts/crosses.
- Then can be linked with spirituality.

### 4.7 Second phalanx of fingers

- **Vertical lines** = **anxiety**.
- **Horizontal lines** (lying flat) on the second phalanx of the **Jupiter finger** = makes you a **researcher** in the Jupiter field.
- Second phalanx quality (student): **processing** — the lines may create trouble in processing, **but** make one a researcher.
- Good for spirituality, **but how far you can step forward** isn't certain → check the **thumb**. If the thumb quality is good and the **Sun finger** is good, one can progress further.

### 4.8 Rare signs

| Sign | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Fish sign** (*matsya*) | Very good for spirituality. Can be on **Jupiter mount**, **Sun mount**, or the **Ketu** mount. (Example: it is in the hand of Dravin ji.) |
| **Lotus** | Very rare; will not look like a perfect lotus; at best a "lotus-like feel". If someone sees one, post it in the group. |
| **Swastika** | Also rare; can appear anywhere, but marks generally appear first **on mounts**. |

> [!NOTE]
> Cut, cross, **til (mole/dot)**, triangle, square are seen in everyone's hands; other signs are rare.

## 6. Hand quality, line density and colour

> [!FAQ] Question to students: "Very few lines in the hand — why is it said?"
> Student answers: "He may not be karma-pradhāna"; "desires may be less"; the teacher corrected that: **few lines does not mean the person will do nothing / get nothing.**

> [!IMPORTANT] Teacher's explanation
> - A **clean hand** shows **happiness** in life.
> - A **hand full of lines** — a **net/web** of lines, on Venus, many lines coming from Mars, going here and there — is also **bad**: the person **overthinks** and **worries too much**; fine lines on fingers and hands = very worried person. A person who worries too much has a different style of bhakti; **fear** sits in the mind.
> - Ideal: **clean hand, good colour, pinkish**.

| Colour | Spiritual implication (as taught) |
|---|---|
| **Pinkish** | Ideal. |
| **Red, with blotches (dhabbe)** | Can take **major steps** in spirituality / bhakti mārga; "that's also not good" (the extreme). |
| **Yellow / white** | Dull/joyless ("nīras"); loneliness; may turn to bhakti **out of distress**; family life may get disturbed → a **balance** issue. |

> [!TIP] Reading method
> Check how the client's **question** comes: whether they are **balancing** both (family + spirituality) or want to **leave home completely and take sannyāsa** — they differ.

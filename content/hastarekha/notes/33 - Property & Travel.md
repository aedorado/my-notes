# Part 1 — Property

## 1.1 First step: understand the question

> [!IMPORTANT] Core principle
> Before reading, see **what kind of question** has come, **whose question** it is, and **in whose hand the sign shows**.
> - The sign often shows in the hand of the person **in whose name the property is**, or **in whose name the loan was taken**.
> - It is not that if the husband bought the property it will not show in the wife's hand, or that if the loan is in the wife's name it will not show in the husband's. **It shows in both.**
> - Even if both are shifting to another house, some indication can still come from somewhere.

## 1.2 Symbols of property

Two symbols were taught: **square** and **triangle**. Both are very good property symbols.

- **Square:** can form on **any line**. Three forms can be seen: (a) a fully closed square, (b) a square formed in another orientation, (c) a square closed from one side only (as drawn: closed on one side). All three give a property indication.
- **Triangle:** on **any line**, a triangle also indicates property.

## 1.3 Lines on which the property indication is found

> [!SUCCESS] Four lines
> | # | Line | Notes |
> |---|---|---|
> | 1 | **Life line** (jīvana rekhā) | Read in **two ways**: by the **sign** on it, and by **expansion** of the life line. Even if the sign is on another line, expansion on the life line is seen. |
> | 2 | **Fate line** (bhāgya rekhā) | Taken because the fate line goes up to the **Saturn finger**, and Saturn is also connected with property. |
> | 3 | **Mercury line** (budha rekhā) | Sign on it. |
> | 4 | **Sun line** (sūrya rekhā) | Sign on it. |

## 1.4 Fate line: side of the sign — residential vs commercial

Fate line taken as going straight (from anywhere). It has **two sides**:

| Side of fate line | Meaning | Reason given |
|---|---|---|
| **Thumb side** (right side as drawn) | **Residential** property — the house/home in which we live | Generally observed so |
| **Mercury-finger side** (left side as drawn) | **Commercial** property | Mercury is connected with commercial matters, and the Mercury line itself lies on this side, so chances of commercial property are higher |

- A triangle or square can form on **either side**; the side decides the kind of property.
- **Both sides** (a square that spans both sides of the line, drawn as such): either **two properties**, or the place where you are has both **residential and commercial** use — "house above, shop below", or you do your work in the same place.

> [!TIP] Fate line origin
> If the **fate line itself starts from Mars or Moon**, chances of **commercial property** also increase **even without a square**, because it is tied to the **career**. Example method: Arvind ji's hand — look there, or ask him whether he has his own shop. Moon being connected with career raises the chances.
> - Some people's **career itself** is property-related (selling, buying); that keeps running in their life.
> - If the **Mars line** is also connected with the career (fate) line, that too can give a property indication.
> - "It will depend on what kind of question my client brings; on that basis we can tell."

## 1.5 Sun line

- Same square/triangle indication applies on the **Sun line**.
- **Thumb side** → **residential**; **Mercury side** → **commercial**.
- Square on both sides → two properties, or residential + commercial in one place (as above).
- **Area/quality of the property:** if the sign is on the **Sun mount**, or the **Jupiter mount** is very good and the Sun mount is very good, the property can be in a **good area** (a "posh area" for the Sun).
  - Teacher: telling the **kind of area** is a bit **difficult**. Look at the **active mount** at the time of the question, or the **life-line expansion** at the age of the question.

> [!EXAMPLE] Expansion reading (as drawn)
> Question comes at a certain age on the life line. Life line was earlier troubled; ahead it is **improving**, quality getting good, moving forward, **smooth, clean**; fate line also looks good. Teacher: "I can say that **from where you were till now, you will go to a better place**." This much can be said.

## 1.6 Mercury line

- Same rule: **thumb side (right)** = **residential** (personal property); **left side** = **commercial**.

## 1.7 Timing: when will property be bought

1. Age is read either on the **life line** or on the **fate line**. Indication will certainly appear on the life line.
2. The sign (square/triangle) occupies **some distance** on the line — the hand does not have exact marks "zero here, 10 years here, 20 here"; a small area on the line spans several years of age.
3. So take the **age span** from where the square/triangle **starts forming** to where it ends: property may get built within that stretch.
4. A **loan** takes **3–4 years or more** to repay; the process of planning and buying also takes time.
5. Tell the client: "From this time you started; after this, **three to four years, or two years**, clear property indications are seen, and the property will be made at this time."

> [!NOTE]
> "Improving indications" are explained next.

## 1.8 Property questions: sale, disputes, loan

### Common questions
- Will it **sell**? At a good price or not?
- A **property dispute** is going on: will it stay with us?
- Will the **loan** be repaid?

### Where disputes come from

| Source | What to check |
|---|---|
| **Ancestral (paitṛka) property** — family, partition, brothers/sisters, money disputes | **Venus mount** (represents family, also in-laws' side) |
| **Enemy lines** | **Lower Mars** (lines come from here) and Mars mount |
| **Venus lines cutting through** | Family disturbance: in-laws, family members, a woman. For example, in a **divorce**, the trouble can be from the **female** side. If lines come from Venus, such trouble is possible. |

### Obstacle method (as drawn)
- Suppose a **triangle** on the fate line at some age ("property will come at this time"), and a **Mars line cuts** across.
- The Mars line "will trouble" it; **the area where it cuts, from the start to the end of the cut — the middle area — has the most trouble.**
- **Look ahead:**
  - If the triangle is **complete/proper** → fine; if only **half** a triangle is visible → **difficulty**.
  - If the line ahead is **smooth** → fine.
  - Take the **same age span** on the **life line**. If the life line is going **well ahead** → fine for property; "disturbances will come in between, but you will get past them".

### Sale question
- Disturbances depend on the question. For **sale**, there may be **difficulty in selling**; the property may sell at a **lower rate** than the one the client wants. *(Transcript wording "rent" for "rate" — [unclear in transcript].)*

### Buying-for-children question — client with Mars lines or negative signs
- Question: "I want to buy and keep a property for the children." If many lines come from **Mars**, or **cut/cross, tail, or any negative sign** is seen → say: **wait; do not buy property at this time, in this age area.**
- If the client **does not agree** (money is ready, "I will buy"):
  - Suggest a **small investment** for a **short time**.
  - **Buy in the good age area before the troubled stretch**, and **sell in that same period** (e.g. "bought and sold right there"). **Skip the middle patch** (the filled area in the drawing).
  - Square with an enemy line coming into it (drawn): advise buying at the earlier age and leaving the middle patch.
- If the client wants to **buy and sell repeatedly**, the same method applies.
- **Possession delays:** if one expects possession to take **4, 5, 6, or 10 years**, the middle patch **will certainly trouble**.

> [!WARNING] Where trouble appears in this hand
> Check **where in the hand the difficulty comes**:
> - **Paperwork** and **government**-related → look at **Mercury (finger)**, **Sun finger**. A **negative mark on the Sun** can be flagged.
> - **Fate line** with a **island** at the same age → "problem will definitely come".
> - Teacher: "We can only **advise** the client; whether the client listens is another matter."

### Loan question: will the loan be repaid?
- Check the **fate line condition**: how clean the square/triangle is.
- Check the **life line** condition ahead.
- A **square at the end of the Saturn mount** (generally at the last part) **helps in repaying the loan**.
- Also look at **Mercury** ("do not forget Mercury"; Mercury is also used for loan).

## 1.9 A house without square or triangle — the supportive Mars line

> [!IMPORTANT] Combination
> A **supportive line (Mars line) running alongside the life line**: if **no triangle or square** is visible, **this is also an indication of property**.
> Conditions: **Venus** and **Jupiter** quality good, and the supportive **Mars line** in good condition, supporting the **fate line**.
> Result: a **beautiful home** — spacious, "open-open": **own roof, own courtyard (āṅgana), outside area also one's own**.

## 1.10 Homework
Students to check **their own hands**: when they bought property, whether any indication is seen, and **in whose name** it was bought.

# Part 2 — Travel (yātrā)

## 2.1 Opening question and discussion

> [!FAQ] Teacher to Dravin ji: how do we understand travel today?
> **Student answer:** Someone travelling for **work**, for long distance, or within **50 km / 100 km**; or going to visit **parents or children** to stay a few days; or going on a **trip** — travel can be seen like this too.
>
> **Teacher's response:** Going out on Saturday-Sunday once a month, or four times a year, is **normal** — everyone has it. It can also be understood by **deśa-kāla** (place/time). Or someone's **work itself** is travel — much "up-down, up-down".

## 2.2 Three kinds of travel (as taught)

| # | Kind | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **By desire/habit** | "We keep travelling; it is our habit." |
| 2 | **Career-linked** | Business or job that includes travel. |
| 3 | **Settling abroad** | Motherland is somewhere else; one settles far away and returns occasionally — every **5, 6, or 10 years**. |

## 2.3 Hand type and travel
- The **desire** for travel is told by **hand type**.
- **Fiery hand** (student answer, teacher confirmed): prefers travel the most — does not want to sit or stay in one place.
- Teacher also says another hand type (transcript reads "ardh hand" — [unclear in transcript]; likely **earthy**) prefers to **connect with roots**; may live outside for a job or work, then come back to his own place.
- Besides hand type, also see the **Moon mount**.

## 2.4 Moon mount
- **Very necessary** to see for travel.
- **Many lines on the Moon mount** (several lines drawn "like this") = **travel lines**, called **yātrā rekhā** in palmistry.
- The **Mercury sister line** (studied earlier) is also a **travel line**.

## 2.5 Career and travel

> [!SUCCESS] Fate-line indications of career-related travel
> 1. **Fate line starts from the Moon mount.**
> 2. Fate line is straight but **a line goes towards the Moon mount** — travel connected with career.
> 3. Fate line **tilting/extending towards the Sun mount** (taking an area that expands toward the Sun) → possibly a **government job** with a lot of **travel**, including **foreign** travel. (Transcript wording "trap" — [unclear in transcript].)

- The hand type can be written along with career when reading this way.

## 2.6 Life line and travel

Two things are seen.

### (a) Expansion / spread of the life line (teacher's own hands)
- **Left hand** (mehndi being applied that day): life line has **large spread**; **Venus area a bit larger**.
- **Dominant hand**: Venus area is a bit **smaller** than in the left, **but many lines on the Moon mount** → travel lines.
- **Small Venus spread** → one **does not accept change quickly**. One travels (family goes, "we must go along"), but if asked "go abroad or stay home?" one **prefers home**. Place, lifestyle, food and drink changes are **not accepted quickly**. Reason given: "the circle is small; **no line goes outward — all lines go inward**" → shrinks into one's own world.
- Even if one goes, **how happy is one there?** Travelling with family (husband goes, wife and children go) can be a **compulsion**. Check this from other things too.
- Hence **lines on the Moon mount alone are not enough**: the **larger the Venus area and the life-line spread**, the more one **steps out of the circle** where thinking, clothing and everything changes, and one **enjoys** it ("not just roaming — enjoy it there").

> [!EXAMPLE] Reading
> Line goes outward → "you will like roaming". **Fiery hand** seen + **life-line spread** seen + **lines on Moon** seen, and the client asks "can I go abroad?" → "Yes, you can." Then **check the quality after that age**.
>
> If **one line is seen coming inward** → "you can go, but your **settlement will be near your motherland**." Example: someone from **UP** may settle in **UP, Delhi, Madhya Pradesh** and nearby; **very far** is not possible. "Go around and come back" — as long as career and money allow.
>
> Teacher: **understanding the spread of the life line is very important.**

### (b) Combinations on the life line (two given)

> [!IMPORTANT] Combination 1 — second (outer) life line starting
> A life line goes (short, to some age) and then either **continues straight**, or **a second life line starts from outside and goes outward**. Teacher saw this in someone's hand.
> - Meaning: **till this age life was elsewhere**; from this age **planning to shift somewhere else begins**; then **growth** follows; one **shifts elsewhere** or **travel increases**.
> - If, in this case, the **fate line** also **joins the life line** and also **starts from the Moon mount**, it indicates travel: where you go, **career starts there**; Moon also indicates travel → **travel gets linked with work/career**.
> - The life line in this example **reaches the Sun mount**. Earlier lesson: for **visa** or **foreign settlement** questions, **the farther the life line goes, the more the person's travel and thinking widen**. In this example it almost comes near the **Moon mount** and goes **fully to the Sun**.

> [!IMPORTANT] Combination 2 — outward line at one age
> Life line curves one way; **fate line goes straight**; no possibility is seen. **At one age, a line goes outward.**
> - **From where the line starts to where it ends = that period** → **expansion**; may go outside.
> - If travel is the question: you will **go and come back; permanent settlement is not seen**; you will be **back by the end age** of that line.

## 2.7 Fate line toward Jupiter
- Fate line goes towards **Jupiter mount**: two ways — (1) a **line leaves the fate line and goes to Jupiter** (on the Jupiter line), since **Jupiter also indicates expansion**; (2) the **fate line itself bends towards Jupiter** — its tilt changes. Either is a **travel indication**.

## 2.8 Passport / visa question
- Look for the indication on the **life line**.
- Look at the **Sun mount** and **Sun finger**.
- "All the planets connected with it, we look at them all."

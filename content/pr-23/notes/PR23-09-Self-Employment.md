## Q&A A – Self-employment / freelancing

> [!FAQ] [Vaibhav] Besides job and business there is **self-employment** (an astrologer, a tuition teacher). How is it understood? (He added that most of his own career has been freelance.)

> [!IMPORTANT] [Teacher] It will be visible in the career reading. To understand it:
> 1. **Start from the person's nature.** The native does not want to work under anyone and does not want to be bound. They want to work on their own terms, for example taking a class at 8:00 or 7:00 as they please. Working under someone means going by that person's terms.
> 2. **The same rule as before:** if there are more planets in the **3rd, 6th, 8th, 12th** houses, this indicates **parivartana (change)**. It shows change in karma, a "journey" of karma. Self-employment is also a journey in this sense: not physical travel, but shifting one's own timing, in one's own way. The person's **parākrama (effort, initiative)** is involved because the 3rd house is counted from the lagna.
> 3. The books use the word *parākrama*. Parākrama can be exercised in any manner and the method may differ. So if the 3rd, 6th, 8th, 12th look more active, this possibility exists. "There is no fixed rule, only a word. Keep extracting from that word and apply it wherever it fits."
>

# Principles Compendium

## 1. Reading method and sequence

**P1. Understand deśa-kāla-paristhiti before concluding**
- **Principle:** Before giving any conclusion, understand the client's place, time and circumstances. If the chart does not show it, ask the client two or four questions.
- **Stated by:** Teacher
- **Shown in:** [[#Chart 1]], [[#Chart 2]], [[#Closing Q&A]]
- **Exceptions / cancellations:** none stated
- **Teacher's reasoning:** Without understanding the client you keep being troubled; the client's own words help.

**P2. Nature first**
- **Principle:** Look at the nature first, with the lagneśa and the mind. "My nature decides my work." (Numerology analogy: the basic number before the destiny number.)
- **Stated by:** Teacher
- **Shown in:** [[#Chart 1]]
- **Exceptions / cancellations:** none stated
- **Teacher's reasoning:** One works as one's nature wants.

**P3. Always see the daśā effect**
- **Principle:** The effect of the running daśā is very strong and can turn everything upside down; never forget it. A direction suggested by the chart is confirmed when the daśā supports it.
- **Stated by:** Teacher
- **Shown in:** [[#Chart 1]], [[#Chart 2]], [[#Chart 3]]
- **Exceptions / cancellations:** none stated

**P4. Give practical advice**
- **Principle:** The client comes for practical advice; advise practically (for example, join the father's work first), not "the Sun's daśā is ahead."
- **Stated by:** Teacher
- **Shown in:** [[#Chart 2]]

**P5. Astrology shows the situation; the person handles it**
- **Principle:** The chart shows the situation; astrology's guidance is how to handle it. The rest will be similar through the daśās; the result depends on how well the person handles it.
- **Stated by:** Teacher
- **Shown in:** [[#Chart 3]]

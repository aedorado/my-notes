# Principles Compendium

## 1. Reading method and sequence

**P1. Deśa-kāla-paristhiti comes first**
- **Principle:** Before reading, take the place, era and circumstance, and what is normal for the community. Marriage problems are common because two people differ in background and thinking. A "delay" is judged by what is normal in that community and era.
- **Stated by:** Teacher
- **Shown in:** [[#Chart 1]], [[#Chart 3]]
- **Exceptions:** none stated
- **Reasoning:** "30 is not a delay now; many people think it is."

**P2. Svabhāva (nature) is the root; sign natures matter**
- **Principle:** Understand sign nature and kālapuruṣa lagna and 7th. Marital problems come "only because of nature" (teacher's view). Guṇa milan matches mentality, love and prakṛti, which are all views on svabhāva. Together with P1 this "solves half the chart".
- **Stated by:** Teacher
- **Shown in:** [[#Chart 1]], [[#Chart 2]], [[#Chart 6]]
- **Exceptions:** none stated

**P3. Answer the client's question; do not recite placements**
- **Principle:** Don't ask or say "your Venus is combust and with X" (many charts have it). Deal with the question asked (e.g. "problem in married life, give a solution"), and do not add your own views. Elders' charts had similar combinations and they managed through nature. Quick questions can be answered via **praśna**, and what is hard from the kuṇḍalī can be answered from praśna ("praśna has more importance"). Praśna lagna can solve this too.
- **Stated by:** Teacher
- **Shown in:** [[#Chart 4]]
- **Exceptions:** none stated

**P4. Ask background before reading**
- **Principle:** Ask date of marriage and the daśā at marriage, whether there are children, whether there is a third party or career issue, who lives together, and how the parties relate to each other's families.
- **Stated by:** Teacher
- **Shown in:** [[#Chart 1]], [[#Chart 3]], [[#Chart 4]]
- **Reasoning:** "Daśā plays a big role"; "easier if no children"; in-laws matter.

## 1. Recap and remaining indications for children

> [!NOTE] Where the teacher stopped last time
> Already taught: the **Jupiter finger** indication and the **Sun finger** indication. *Sun finger long → possibility of a male child* (repeated as a recap).

### 1.1 Saturn finger (madhyamā) — middle phalanx
- Mostly to be checked in the **female** hand.
- A **cross** on the **middle phalanx (parva) of the Saturn finger** → also an indication of **no child**.
- **Do not conclude "no child" from this sign alone.** All other conditions of the hand and the **quality of the hand** must also be checked.
- Practical statement for such a case: say the person **needs medical help** (for **both** partners), rather than telling anyone directly "no child".

### 1.2 Branches on the child line

| Formation | Meaning |
|---|---|
| A child line from which **one branch** goes **downward** (or one goes **upward**) | **Twins (juṛwā santān)** |
| The same condition on **both sides** (downward and upward) | Same indication; also **more than one child at one time** |
| Branch from a branch from a branch… (can go on any distance) | **Many children together** — e.g. six or seven at once (as read in newspapers) |

> [!WARNING] Common mistake
> "More than one child" here does **not** mean two separate lines. Two lines = two children (taught earlier). Here **one line gives off another line**, i.e. **at the same time**.

- Twins also carries a second indication: **excess of some body part** in the child — e.g. children born with two heads, an extra finger, extra toes (teacher mentioned "30 fingers"). Exactly *what* will be is difficult to say.
- Statement from the books (as the teacher reports): the books say **"puruṣa santān" [unclear in transcript]**, which the teacher takes to mean a **healthy child**.
  - Not necessary that the child lives long.
  - For a child line of this kind (branching/this chapter's formation) **no guarantee of health can be given**; the teacher **does not consider it good** for children.

## 2. Quality of the child line

> [!IMPORTANT] Principle
> Judge the **quality (colour/darkness/depth)** of the child line as with other lines. Lines should **not be very dark**.

### 2.1 Reading by darkness (reference demonstration on the case hand's heart line)
- Heart line of the case hand: should match the **colour of the hand**; here the colour looked "completely different" and the line broad. Teacher's remark (also noted the previous day): she **takes stress** and her **health, especially heart health**, needs watching.
- At first glance the hand also looked **male-dominant (male-hand quality)** though it is a female hand.
- The child line need **not be dark throughout**: it may start very thin and become dark later.

| Pattern along the child line | Teacher's reading |
|---|---|
| **Light at the start, dark later** | The line start = **birth/initial age of the child**. Child may have been **weak at birth** / health trouble (e.g. stomach upset); health improved as age grew. (Teacher's own wording is mixed here; the idea: *starting heavy/problem → later improves*) |
| **Very dark at the start** | Trouble in the child's health at the start; improves later if the line then becomes normal/light |
| **Very dark → then light** | Prediction differs from "normal dark then healthy" |
| **Normal dark, then healthy** | Different prediction again |

> [!FAQ] Practice question to Harish ji
> **Q (Teacher):** A line that is very dark at the start and then becomes light — what would you say?
> **A (Harish ji):** Everything fine at first, then the person may fall ill later.
> **Teacher's correction:** Do not say "falls ill" until you see the **condition of the rest of the line**. In this case the line is **very dark**, and very dark itself is bad → trouble in health **at the start**; then see how the line goes onward.

### 2.2 Symbols on lines — teacher's rules
- Any **negative mark** (island, cross, cut, a sudden stop/break of the line) is to be noted on any line.
- **Cross → good only on the Jupiter mount.** Nowhere else.
- **Island → good only on the family ring.** Nowhere else.
- All other impure signs remain impure wherever in the palm they appear.
- **Triangle and square** → most positive; very common, easy to find. Cross is also easy to find.
- **Rare signs:** swastika, lotus, canopy; **trident** (also not in everyone's hand).

> [!EXAMPLE] Examples from this class's students (Anurag to use as sign examples in the notes/tool)
> - **Dravin ji** — trident on the **Sun finger/Sun line** (very clearly visible).
> - **Arvind ji** — a **big triangle**.
> - **Anuradha ji** — **girdle of Venus** (generally rare in hands).
> - Plan: put one example per sign; highlighting such signs makes a good example set.

## 4. Digression — Rings on the fingers (teacher's explanation)

### 4.1 Rule on what counts as a symbol
- A line that merely **passes through** another line's **path** (e.g., a fate line crossing between heart and head lines) is **not a cross/symbol**. A symbol must be looked at separately and **not be in a line's path**.
- A Sun line crossing a finger ring: not a cross.

### 4.2 Girdle of Venus
- A circle from one point to another; need not be complete; small pieces also count.
- The better it is, the better its qualities; can make the person **creative**.
- Dark patches, cuts, crosses, islands reduce its quality.

### 4.3 Ring under each finger

| Ring | Good quality | Bad quality / note |
|---|---|---|
| **Saturn (under Saturn finger)** | Researcher; spiritual; good for research and spirituality | **Introvert**, lonely nature, isolation, **headstrong**, very practical; society-avoiding. Also check **heart line**: if the heart line ends at Saturn, worse; if it reaches Jupiter, less problem; **watery hand** makes person emotional |
| **Sun/Apollo ring** | Creativity; talent; name and fame | **Desire for name/fame** too strong; **stubbornness/ego**. Desire is good only up to a limit |
| **Mercury ring** | Business mind (self-benefit) | Rare. **Lower moral values**; tendency toward **fraud** (like the numerology "nine, double/triple five" type); risky sexual behaviour; in a male, mindset toward females changes. If the **thumb** is a **murderer's thumb**, **Jupiter finger not tilted toward Saturn**, and **Mars very active** → can fall in the **rapist** category. Not good for relationships; it reflects family life and creativity too |

## 8. Summary

> [!SUMMARY] Key takeaways
> - Saturn finger middle-phalanx **cross** → indication of no child — but never alone; advise **medical help**.
> - **One branch (up/down)** from a child line → twins; **branches of branches** → many children at once; **extra body part** may come with it.
> - Child-line **quality**: not too dark; start vs later darkness shows the child's health trajectory.
> - Cross good only on **Jupiter mount**; island good only on **family ring**.
> - Life-line **outward branch** = external support; **inward** = responsibility; for a childbirth question, outward = **external/medical help**.
> - Check **Mars, Venus, Mercury, Jupiter** and **attachment** before judging childbirth.
> - **Bracelet lines**: 1 health, 2 wealth, 3 happiness, 4 samrāṭ yoga; five = poverty; don't rely on them.
> - Rings: Saturn (research/isolation), Sun (creativity/ego), Mercury (business/immorality).
> - Nails: lunula absence, vertical lines, yellowish colour, short/bitten nails → check-up.

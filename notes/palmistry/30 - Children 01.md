# Santāna Rekhā (Child Line) 01

## 1. Before reading the child line: deśa-kāla-paristhiti (place, time, circumstance)

- Many things must be understood before reading the child line. First, **deśa, kāla, paristhiti** (country, time, circumstance).
- The śāstra describes everything, but as in astrology: even when the **fifth house looks good, problems still appear** at times. The same attitude is carried into palmistry.
- **Examine the hands of both male and female** (husband and wife), not just one.

### 1.1 Hand quality first
> [!TIP] First thing to look at
> Look at the **quality of the hand** first. "Never skip it." Hand quality reveals many things.

- **Nails** → show **health**. Health issues that can disturb conception: e.g. **thyroid**, **PCOD / PCOS** etc. may lead to problems in conception.
- **Tension / stress** is the biggest factor. Today stress begins at a young age (heavy studies, career), and child birth means taking on **responsibility**, which itself creates tension.

### 1.2 Society has changed
- Earlier concept: after marriage, a child within one or two years was assumed. Now: "first career", reluctance to take responsibility.
- Medical science has grown: **preserving (eggs/embryos)**, **IVF**, **surrogacy**.
- Result: the chance of **child's death has become lower**, but **conception-related matters have changed completely**.
- Practical consequence for palmistry: lines in **older hands** may appear as in the texts, but **upcoming (younger) hands will show many changes**.

## 2. Mounts to examine before the child line

| Mount | What it shows here | Teacher's reading |
|---|---|---|
| **Śukra parvata (Venus mount)** | Family and **desire** | If there is no desire, a child-line is of little use. In the **male**, any cut, cross or mole on Venus → problem in conception **from the male side**. |
| **Candra parvata (Moon mount)** | Mind; the **power to conceive** | Examined especially in the **female**. If disturbed (e.g. mole, mark) → chances of **miscarriage / abortion** rise slightly. |
| **Maṅgala (Mars): outer and inner** | **Action** | Planning a child is an act of action; the child-birth process starts only after it. **Inner Mars** = mental action; **outer Mars** area = external action. Both must be good. |
| **Budha parvata (Mercury mount)** | **Reproductive / sex organs** of both male and female | The better the Mercury, the fewer physical problems. Also see the **Mercury line** (it shows health). |

> [!SUMMARY] Teacher's summary of the four
> Venus = our **desire**; Moon = our **mind** (female especially); Mars = our **action**; Mercury = our **organs**. A **collective study** is needed.

- Whichever hand is under study (husband or wife), where the problem appears, assume **from whose side** the problem comes. In child birth the problem need not be in both; it may be in one, in both, or in neither.

### 2.1 Responsibility lines (recalled from life line)
- From the earlier lesson: lines going **inward from the jīvana rekhā (Life Line)** = **responsibilities taken**.
- After marriage, with career already running, the responsibility can be **a child** or **the husband's family** (e.g. "I will look after mother-in-law, no need for a maid").
- So the child-birth indication can also appear as a **responsibility line**. It need **not be permanent**: it may appear for some time and go. Mother's workload is higher while children are small; in some cases the father's too. It can be seen in the mother's hand.

## 3. Indication 1 — Child lines above the marriage line

- The earliest indication of child birth is found on the **vivāha rekhā**. These lines are **very fine**; one may need to apply ink or use a **lens** to see them.

### 3.1 Which lines to count
1. **Downward lines** from the marriage line: last class these were discussed to be **disturbance lines** of married life. **Do not count them** as child lines.
2. **Only upward lines** (going up, not coming down) above the marriage line are child lines.
3. **Small, short lines**: **do not count**.
4. Count only the **clear-cut, long, standing line(s)**.
5. Where many lines (light, dark) appear, do not get confused: remove downward ones, remove short ones, then take the **upward branches**; among them (short and long) take the **long** ones.

> [!IMPORTANT] What this indication gives
> It gives only **yogyatā (capacity/possibility)** of child birth, **not the number**. ("Today, say one or two, at most three, not more.")

> [!NOTE] Gender indication 
> A **slightly darker/thicker** line indicates a **male child**; a **light** line indicates a **female child** (stated as being said in the śāstras).
> More than one line can also indicate **miscarriage** and other possibilities (teacher noted this from her own hand: she has more than one line).

### 3.2 The grid method (Mercury mount division)
> [!EXAMPLE] 
> - Take the Mercury mount area. Divide it **vertically into 4 parts and horizontally into 4 parts** (3 internal dividing lines each way).
> - The teacher marked a portion of this grid (in blue) as the area to consider. The **marriage line must reach at least into this area**; child lines are the lines that **begin inside this marked area** (going beyond is a different matter).
> - Purpose: avoid confusion with the **health line** and the **marriage line** in the same region.
> - She added that this **parts-division was used earlier** and is not used now; now one simply looks for **long, clear lines**, and states **one or two** children.

## 4. Indication 2 — The family ring

> [!INFO] Family ring 
> The ring that connects the thumb's phalanges area and the thumb's mount, i.e. the ring at the base of the thumb that separates the **thumb** from the **Venus mount**. There is a **bone** near it (thumb side) and another **bone** on the Venus-mount side. The family ring marks a **bifurcation**: it separates the thumb from Venus mount. 

- The second indication appears (1) **on the family ring** and (2) in the **area above the family ring up to the thumb-side bone**.

![Fig 2. Example Shown, Has 2 Kids & 1 Miscarriage](/lecture-notes-images/30/02-example.png)

### 4.1 Island on the family ring
- A marking that elsewhere is called an **island** is **negative elsewhere** but **very good on the family ring**.
- **Number of clear islands = number of children** (it does not tell male/female).
- An island must be **clear-cut**. If many islands but heavily **cut/crossed**, they do **not** indicate child birth.
- Islands also show **attachment**: if there is no island-like marking on the family ring, it shows **less attachment (juḍāv)** to the children ("children are born, fine, we'll pay their fees; no bond").
- **Chain-type formation counts as island**: Student (unnamed) asked; Teacher: yes, **chain is island** (confirmed).

### 4.2 Lines between the family ring and the thumb-side bone
- In the area from the family ring up to the bone, count the **upward vertical lines**: they indicate the **number of children**.
- Only **clear lines** count (teacher: "I do not care for the small ones").
- Shown on the teacher's example hand: **three clear lines**; she explained that the woman has **two children and one miscarriage**. So the number of lines can also reflect a miscarriage. We can see 2 lines are complete and one is broken.
- The teacher said many books say to look for these lines **in the female hand only**, since only the female has the power to bear a child; but **in practice we can take both hands**.

**Student (Anuradha-ji)**: one prominent line and one smaller; two present.

### 4.3 Summary of number indications (as taught)
1. **Number of clear islands on family ring.**
2. **Number of clear upward lines between family ring and bone.**
3. Practical statement to a client: one or two.

> [!NOTE] Teacher's recap
> First: **possibility** (marriage-line upward lines). Second: **how many children** (islands on family ring, lines between ring and bone). When a client asks "how many children?", we generally tell from the **islands**, as clearly as they appear.

## 5. Sibling lines (bhāī-bahan rekhā)

![Fig 3. Siblings Line](/lecture-notes-images/30/03-siblings-lines.png)

- Area: **below the family ring**, from the family ring down to the **Venus mount** side, up to where the **life line** joins; there is a **bone** here. The **upward lines** rising from here are **sibling lines**.
- Because **Śukra (Venus) mount represents the whole family**, these lines need not mean only blood siblings.
- Some lines may be **very prominent**; others small, broken, starting and stopping, or with marks. The prominent lines can also represent **cousin brothers/sisters** with whom one has more attachment.
- The more attachment to someone, the more clearly the line appears.

> [!EXAMPLE] Teacher's own hand
> Three prominent lines; she has **three siblings** (four siblings in total), and the lines that start, break and continue show people she has had occasional contact with.

> [!EXAMPLE] Student (Anuradha-ji): many sibling lines
> - **Observed:** very many lines. 
> - **Facts given by student:** 3 siblings and about **28 cousins** in all (father had 5 brothers and 2 sisters, each with 3–4 children; mother's side: 3 siblings, each with 2 children). Close-knit family.
> - **Teacher's reading:** very many lines → either one is **very concerned/attached**; fits the close family.

### 5.1 Girdle of Venus ("Griddle of Venus")
- Seen on the Anuradha Ji's hand: starts in the area between **two lines** (the area between the **Mercury finger and Sun finger** to between **Jupiter finger and Saturn finger**), forming a **ring-like shape**.
- Meaning: **over-sensitive**, can become **possessive** and **controlling** (trying to control others' lives); **over-protective**; also makes the person **creative**.
- Whether positive or negative depends on other factors, e.g. **hand quality** (a hard-type hand adds a controlling nature).
- The student had this ring.

## 6. Yogas and markings

![Fig 3. Siblings Line](/lecture-notes-images/30/04-cross-on-child-line.png)

### 6.1 Garbhapāta yoga (miscarriage / abortion yoga)
- A **small, fine cross** (or star-like mark) at the **start** of a child line (on the marriage line) = **garbhapāta yoga**. Indicates **abortion / miscarriage**, in the **initial months**.
- If the cross is at the **top end** of the child line: consider **no child** (no possibility); even if conception happens, one may **carry the pregnancy for some time**, but a problem can come in the **8th–9th month**.
- If a **cross** forms and a **line joins** it, per the śāstra this indicates **akāla mṛtyu**: the child is **born, but later suffers an untimely/accidental death**.

### 6.2 Dattaka putra yoga (adopted child)
- Why adoption: either the person **does not want** a child (new-generation, especially young females who fear the long process / body being spoiled), or child birth is **not happening**.
- Conditions: **Śani parvata (Saturn mount)** slightly **raised** (earlier lesson: Saturn is better lower, but not a deep pit, which causes trouble in career) + **upper Mars** slightly raised + **a line joining** the two → **dattaka putra yoga**.
- Teacher's note: texts only say *putra* (son) because of a male-dominated society; astrology does the same.
- Further combination: **many crosses on Venus mount** → trouble in married life → child-birth problem (general observation).

## 7. Indication — Timing and the responsibility line

- Look at the **life line after marriage**: **inward branches** (**responsibility lines**) or a **thin line running alongside** the life line (called **parasite line**; teacher noted we obviously do not call a child a parasite, but the line can be considered).
- A child's responsibility is large. Abroad it ends in a few years; **in India it continues** even when the children are married with their own children.
- **Soft hand** → stronger attachment to the child. **Family ring in good condition** → strong attachment; parents keep worrying even after children have children.

> [!IMPORTANT] Lines can disappear with mindset
> - If the parent **stops** the responsibility (child is settled, married with children; "now I will live my life"), the **old lines fade**.
> - Teacher's own example: an **island on her Mercury line** (health) appeared, with a small disturbance on the life line at that time; the health issue lasted **6–7 months** but the island stayed **one year** after, meaning the illness was lingering; for the last **4–5 months** the island has been **disappearing**, i.e. the body has fully recovered.
> - Lines change **only through our mindset**. **Manifestation**: take photos of the hand, keep a strong intention day and night; impressions begin to show in **3–5 months** (any remedy takes this time). She suggested trying it with one's own hand.

## 9. More indications

### 9.1 Mercury finger and mount
- **Short Mercury finger** → **low interest**: low interest in children and **low interest in physical relation/intimacy** with partner. (if finger is much shorter than Sun Finger i.e. much below Sun's top phalange)
- Look at the **Mercury finger alone** only when it is odd compared with the rest of the hand (if all fingers are thin, do not judge one).
- **Mole** on Mercury mount → not good. **Jālī (grille)** on Mercury mount (three lines this way, three that way, clear, **not connected** to the marriage line) → not good.
- **Smaller mount area** → trouble in child birth. How does the mount's extent become small? The **joint line** (where the ME finger connects the palm). A **low-set finger** (the line reaches into the mount, making the area smaller).

### 9.2 Jupiter (Guru) mount and finger
- **Putra kāraka** is Jupiter. Check the **whole Jupiter finger** and **Jupiter mount**.
- If the Jupiter finger has a problem → trouble regarding **male child**; teacher: books differ slightly on this; she will say more later.
- Jupiter mount in **good condition** → no trouble in child birth.

> [!FAQ] Draviṇ-ji: Should conditions be seen in male or female or mainly the female? 
> A: **Both.** Moon mount is given more weight in the **female** because it shows the **power of conception**; the rest are seen in both.

### 9.3 Marriage line turning/curving upward
- First: indication of **no marriage**. If married: shows **no interest** in the partner. If no interest in married life → "forget child birth".

### 9.4 Life line and infertility
- **Short life line** → **infertility**. If it stops **before reaching Venus mount**, i.e. it ends at **lower Mars**, it is an infertility sign.
- Applicable to **male and female**; the teacher related it to the **absence of the Y chromosome**.
- Check the **age** at which the life line ends.

### 9.5 Venus mount
- Condition must be good (restated).

### 9.6 Sun finger vs Jupiter finger (gender / count sūtra)
- View the hand from the **front side**. Compare **only Sun (Sūrya) and Jupiter** fingers; **not** with Saturn.

| Condition | Indication |
|---|---|
| **Both equal in length** | Either **two sons or two daughters**; two or four children, equal in number (two sons, two daughters; or one son, one daughter) |
| **Sun finger longer than Jupiter** | Greater possibility of a **male child** |
| **Jupiter finger longer than Sun** | **Female child** condition |

- Reasoning: in astrology Jupiter indicates a male child, but by **Kālapuruṣa** the Sun represents the male child; so Sun longer → male child.
- **Not to be used alone** to declare male/female; it is a **sūtra** (given for knowledge).

## 10. Summary (family ring vs sibling area)

> - **Between the thumb and the Venus mount**, a line runs: the **family ring**.
> - **On the family ring**: islands. Number of **clear islands = number of children**.
> - **Between the family ring and the thumb-side bone**: **vertical upward lines** → **number of children**.
> - **Between the family ring and the bone on the Venus side (toward the life line)**: upward lines → **sibling lines** (younger and elder siblings).
> - There are two bones: one on the thumb, and one on the Venus mount side.
> - Teacher asked the student to practice more on this.

## 2. Nature of signs in the houses
> [!IMPORTANT] Core rule
> - **Kendra (angular houses) 1, 4, 7, 10 → all four are of the same nature (movable/cara).**
> - Likewise **2, 5, 8, 11 → all fixed (sthira)**; **3, 6, 9, 12 → all dual (dvisvabhāva)**.
> - **Trikoṇa (trines) → each trine contains all three natures.** With Aries lagna: 1st movable, 5th fixed, 9th dual.
> - This is true for **any lagna**, not only Aries.

- Teacher: because of this, when a kendra and trikoṇa are related, the "positive and negative go together", and results are better. (Explained later.)
- Trines in detail (board): 1-5-9 = movable, fixed, dual; 2-6-10 = fixed, dual, movable; 3-7-11 = dual, movable, fixed; 4-8-12 = movable, fixed, dual. So a trikoṇa carries **all three guṇas/natures**.
- **Q (Chaitanya Chaudhary):** Why does a trine have three natures while a kendra has one? **A:** There is a hidden reason; it will be explained when kendra/trikoṇa signs are taught.
- **Q (Gaurav / unnamed student):** For the larger triangle (7 and 11) what applies? **A:** The three houses of a trikoṇa are of three different natures.

## 4. Bhāvāt bhāvam (from a house, to the same house counted from it)

> [!INFO] Definition
> Count from the house of a significator to the same-numbered house from it. The result of the *derived* house also speaks about the same matter. Applied **once only**.

- Examples taught on the board: children — the 5th house; the 5th **from** the 5th is also about children. Happiness — the 4th, and the 4th from the 4th. Longevity — the 8th and the 8th from the 8th. Karma — the 10th and the 10th from the 10th.
- **12th from a house = the expense/loss of that house.** The 12th from lagna is lagna's vyaya; the 12th from the 7th is the 7th's vyaya; the 12th from the 9th is the 9th's vyaya.
- **Māraka houses:** the 8th is āyu, and the 12th from it is the **7th house**. The teacher counted the 12th from an āyu house twice on the board (the second āyu house is not named in the transcript), and concluded: *this is the 2nd place and this is the 7th place — the **2nd and 7th are called māraka**.*
- **Mother:** the 4th stands for mother. Treat the 4th as "lagna" for mother — the 12th from it shows loss to the mother; the 4th from the 4th gives the mother's happiness; the 3rd from the 4th gives the mother's strength (bal), which comes from siblings, so the **mother's siblings — maternal uncles — are seen from the 6th**.
- Teacher: this is why the one-word set must be remembered for each of the twelve houses: it lets us go into the detail of any chart or any other house.
- The effect of the derived house is not equal to the original house but it is there.

> [!FAQ] Questions
> **Q (Student):** Is bhāvāt bhāvam done once or repeatedly? **A:** Once only.
> **Q (Vikas Mishra):** For the 2nd, what is "second from second"? **A:** The **3rd**.
> **Q (Student):** Why was the count made twice for karma? **A:** Teacher: we went into the 5th and counted 5th from it; into the 4th and counted the 4th from it — this is bhāvāt bhāvam.
> **Q (Nini):** Is the effect of bhāvāt bhāvam equal to the original house? **A:** Not equal, but the effect is there.
> *The teacher ended the topic quickly when students interrupted; he said earlier that it gives "a great deal about the prediction part".*

## 5. Kendra and trikoṇa: how to find them quickly

> [!INFO] Sets (the teacher's "three kendras" and "four trikoṇas")
> **Kendra sets:** 1-4-7-10 · 2-5-8-11 · 3-6-9-12. **Trikoṇa sets:** 1-5-9 · 2-6-10 · 3-7-11 · 4-8-12.
> The kendra distance is "from anywhere: 1, 4, 7, 10".

- If the lord of the 6th is placed in the 6th's own kendra set, say: "the 6th lord has gone to a kendra **from itself**". Likewise a planet in the 2nd house for the 5th: the 2nd lord in the 5th = kendra from itself.
- **Quick method without counting:** a planet in the 1st: the other trine houses of 1-5-9 give its trikoṇa; the teacher spoke it as "5th and 1st" for a planet in the 9th, and as "6th and 2nd" for a planet in the 10th. (See Open items: slip in what was said for the planet in the 1st.)
- **Check every planet from two places:** from **lagna** and from its **own house/bhāva**. From lagna we remember the numbers (2nd, 10th, 11th, 12th…), but from the bhāva we must know whether it is a kendra or a trikoṇa without counting.

> [!EXAMPLE] Brijbhushan's chart (shown for this method)
> - **Chart:** Libra lagna. Venus, Mars, Sun in the 12th. Moon and Mercury in lagna. Jupiter in the 5th. Saturn in the 9th. Rahu in the 2nd. Ketu in the 8th.
> - **Teacher's reading of Mars:** Mars is in the 12th from lagna; **from its own house** (Mars was called "dvitīya" — the 2nd) it is in the **11th**. If Mars had been in the 5th house, from lagna it would be in a trikoṇa, but from its own (2nd) house it would be in a **kendra** — "this is what I want to teach".
> - **Reasoning:** examine a planet from lagna *and* from its own house.

## 7. Method of getting results: bhāva + dātā graha

> [!IMPORTANT] The teacher's core method
> Keep the circle **as small as possible**. A result depends on **two things only: the bhāva and the dātā graha (giver planet).**
> - **For the bhāva** look at **three things:** the **bhāva**, the **bhāveśa** (its lord) and the **sthira kāraka** (fixed significator).
> - **For the dātā graha** look at **only the planet** that gives the result.
> - Go deeper only if you cannot understand the chart. *"There cannot be a smaller circle than this."* He will not ask students to see more.

> [!EXAMPLE] Teacher's examples
> - **Speech (vāṇī):** the 2nd house, the 2nd lord, the 2nd's sthira kāraka (Jupiter); the dātā graha is **Mercury**. Without Mercury we cannot say much about speech.
> - **Wife for a man:** house 7, dātā graha **Venus**.
> - **Husband for a woman:** house 7, dātā graha **Jupiter**.
> - **Bhāgya:** the 9th house; dātā graha **Jupiter**.
> - **Father:** dātā graha **Sun**. The sthira kāraka of the 9th is still Jupiter.

- **Dātā graha** is a **kāraka**, either a **sthira kāraka (fixed)** or a **cara kāraka (changing)**. The teacher coined/uses the term *dātā graha* to avoid saying "find the cara kāraka": **"who is the giver?"**
- **Sthira kāraka = fixed significator = "achara"**; there is **no dual (dvisvabhāva)** among kārakas.
- The teacher later said: take the **dātā graha as the cara kāraka**; there is no list of cara kārakas to memorize — it will become obvious with practice.
- **Q (Student):** For the father do we see both Jupiter and Sun? **A (Teacher):** For the 9th the sthira kāraka is Jupiter (bhāva, bhāveśa, sthira kāraka), and the dātā graha is the Sun.
- **Q (Student):** For marriage Venus is always the kāraka, for both male and female? **A:** Venus is the kāraka. In a girl's chart the dātā graha becomes Jupiter. *"What is the problem?"*
- **Q (Raunak):** the earlier playlist mentions the Kālapuruṣa chart's natural lord of the house; what is its role? **A:** We will take its role when giving results; two things are enough.

### Teacher's pedagogical stance
- Rule to be held "like a vajra" (unbreakable): even after 12–20 years of study the result comes **only from bhāva and dātā graha**.
- "Do not go so far" — a student asked whether, for longevity loss (8th, with the 12th from it as the 7th), the 12th must also be seen. Teacher: only what has been taught; otherwise the shortcut rule will start breaking.

> *Sthira kārakas do not change; the dātā graha changes with the question.*

## 10. Dṛṣṭi (aspects)

### 10.1 Which houses each planet aspects
- **All planets** aspect the **7th** house (Sun too; **Rahu and Ketu included**).
- **Mars** additionally: **4th and 8th**. **Jupiter** additionally: **5th and 9th**. **Rahu–Ketu** additionally: **5th and 9th**. **Saturn** additionally: **3rd and 10th**.
- Rahu and Ketu aspect each other's 7th (yes).
- Asked why Rahu/Ketu (Saturn-like/Mars-like in nature) do not have Saturn's or Mars's special aspects: teacher: *as taught in the scriptures; I have taught it as it is.*
- Rahu/Ketu have no physical existence ("no head, no body"); even so their aspects have effect.

### 10.2 Nature of an aspect — the teacher's simplified approach
> [!IMPORTANT] Rules (the teacher says he departs from the usual astrological society here)
> 1. **Natural benefics' aspects are auspicious; natural malefics' aspects are inauspicious.**
> 2. **Temporary (tātkālika) lordship and placement do not affect an aspect.** E.g. Jupiter as the 8th lord, or a planet in the 8th, does not spoil its aspect.
> 3. Therefore the ideas of **exalted-planet aspect, debilitated-planet aspect, friend's aspect are "rubbish" (bakwās)**. Such things are written in *Bhṛgu Saṃhitā*; the teacher advises tying the book in red cloth and putting it high, out of reach. Reason: a debilitated planet cannot give an "exalted aspect".
> 4. **Mercury:** auspicious when alone; becomes inauspicious **with inauspicious planets**, and then its aspects are inauspicious. (Student: whenever Budha-Āditya yoga forms, Mercury is inauspicious. Teacher: yes.)
> 5. **Moon:** inauspicious when within **72° ahead of or behind the Sun**; otherwise auspicious. An auspicious Moon's aspects are auspicious.
> 6. When Moon or Mercury is inauspicious, they join in forming **pāpa kartarī**.
> 7. **Rahu/Ketu** are malefic even when alone (a student asked; the teacher said yes).
> 8. The Sun, Mars and Saturn are **undisputedly inauspicious**; Jupiter and Venus **undisputedly auspicious**.

### 10.3 Kartarī
- **Pāpa kartarī:** inauspicious planets in the houses **on both sides** of a house or planet. **Śubha kartarī:** auspicious planets in the previous and next house.
- **Jagannātha's view** (the teacher refers to a Jagannātha scholar): if the middle house has no benefic and an inauspicious planet is two houses ahead, it is still pāpa kartarī. **Teacher does not accept this**: only the **immediately adjacent** houses count (e.g., with a planet in the 4th, the 3rd and 5th). If the 5th is empty and the 7th has a malefic, it is not kartarī.
- **Q (Vishal):** If a planet is at the extreme degree (29°) of the adjacent house, does it change the śubha kartarī? **A:** No. Only the **house** matters, whatever the degree.

### 10.4 Manner of harm by each malefic (illustration)
- Sun: draws the sword and cuts the head, no mercy. Mars: breaks legs, hands and head, then kills. Saturn: quietly mixes poison. **The harm follows the planet's nature; the same for aspects.**
- The teacher: a general illustration; do not take it too seriously.

### 10.5 What an aspect does
- An aspect first **activates the house**, and second gives **results**. Results depend on two options: **as it was**, or **transformed** (e.g., Saturn aspecting the wealth house — will it destroy wealth or give wealth? How the result transforms comes later).
- **Aspects influence living beings more:** 4th mother, 3rd brother/sibling, 2nd family, 6th maternal uncle, 7th spouse, 8th in-laws, 9th father, 10th colleagues. Example: a 5th house aspected by Saturn, Sun and Mars will not allow a child; if a child is born it will give suffering to the child. (Analogy: being scolded or even beaten is borne, but being paid ₹500 instead of ₹1,000 hurts; what is felt depends on what is affected.)
- **Effect falls on the house and on planets seated in it — not on the house lord.** Analogy: if you see a house with an elephant and a horse, you see both; a third animal sitting elsewhere is not seen.
- **Retrograde planets:** the aspect is just the same.
- **Daśā:** a planet need not be in its daśā to give an aspect; the aspect is always there.
- **Q (Gaurav):** Does Rahu/Ketu's 5th/9th aspect have 100%? **A:** Yes, 100% on the 5th and 9th.

### 10.6 Pāda (quarter) strengths — taught for knowledge
> [!INFO] Table
> | Pāda | Strength | Applies to |
> |---|---|---|
> | 1st | **25%** | **3rd and 10th** from the planet by **any planet except Saturn** |
> | 2nd | **50%** | **5th and 9th** by **any planet except Jupiter** (called the *trine effect*) |
> | 3rd | **75%** | **4th and 8th** by **any planet except Mars** |
> | 4th | **100%** | 7th by every planet; **Mars** 4th and 8th; **Jupiter** 5th and 9th; **Saturn** 3rd and 10th |

- **"Pāda" here is the quarter of an aspect**, not the nakṣatra pāda (Q by a student; teacher: *not nakṣatra pāda; say pāda, pada or 25%, all are fine*).
- **Every planet therefore has seven aspects** (a student, new to this, noted he had thought "maximum three"). Example given for Mars: 3rd (25%), 4th (100%), 5th (50%), 7th (100%), 8th (100%), 9th (50%), 10th (25%).
- Demonstrations on the board: **Saturn** — 5th/9th 50%, 7th 100%; **Jupiter** — 5th/7th/9th 100%; **Mercury** (any non-Jupiter) — 5th and 9th 50%, 7th 100%; **Mars** — 4th, 7th, 8th 100%; **Saturn (non-Mars)** 4th/8th 75%; **Venus** — 4th and 8th 75%, 7th 100%; **Mars (a non-Saturn planet)** 3rd/10th 25%, 7th 100%; **Jupiter** 3rd/10th 25%, 7th 100%.
- **Q (Raunak):** Will the aspect fall if a planet in the 1st is at 2° and one in the 7th at 5°? **A:** Yes it falls; this is calculation, learnt in the schedule part.
- **Q (Chaitanya Chaudhary):** Mars and Saturn both in lagna; Mars has 100% and Saturn 75% on the 4th; enemies, so what result? **A (Teacher):** The *effect* will be taught later; today we learn how the aspect is applied. *Mars 100% plus Saturn (also inauspicious) 75% is "dangerous"* (the student's comment; teacher: yes).
- **Q (Student):** Is there a mathematical (degree-wise) form? **A:** In the schedule; for now this is enough.
- **Q (Gaurav):** If Jupiter is also there with the malefics? **A:** It reduces inauspicious results; ṣaḍbala is not taught (it requires gaṇita; takes about six months; learn it elsewhere).
- **Q (Sama):** Do aspects hold for retrograde planets? **A:** The same. **Q:** If two planets (Jupiter and Saturn) are in lagna, do we add or subtract? **A:** The teacher: "I will handle all" (cricket analogy: a batsman handles spin, pace and bouncers; handling comes later).

## 11. Kendrīya prabhāva (central influence)

> [!IMPORTANT] Definition
> A planet in any house influences the four kendra houses from itself: **1, 4, 7, 10 from itself.** This comes out of the pāda system.
> | From the planet | Strength | By |
> |---|---|---|
> | Its own house | its own full presence | — |
> | 4th from it | **75%** | 3rd-pāda (4th aspect) |
> | 7th from it | **100%** | 4th-pāda (7th aspect) |
> | 10th from it | **25%** | 1st-pāda (3rd/10th aspect) |
>
> A malefic in a kendra disturbs its four kendras; a benefic improves them.

- Most teachers teach only the **"10th effect"** (e.g., "Mars in the 10th from the Moon"): a planet in the 10th looks at lagna with the 3rd pāda (75%). The teacher teaches the **full kendrīya prabhāva** instead; the trine (50%) effect he teaches in transits (gocara).
- **Saturn example (board):** Saturn disturbs all four of its kendras (including 100% on the 7th and 100% on the 10th; 75% on the 4th). **Mars** in a kendra: 100%, 100%, 25% and 100% on the various houses; Sun's pattern 75%, 100%, 25% and 100%. **Saying:** *Jupiter in lagna removes a lakh doshas; Saturn in lagna "loads" a lakh doshas.*
- **Example:** Sun, Mars and Saturn in the 4th. Only Mars aspects the 7th fully; but the Sun and Saturn also act at **75% each** — so they also "put hands and feet" in marital life. (The teacher: apply this for knowledge, not as a mathematical formula.)
- A malefic mass in lagna (Mars 100% + Sun/Saturn 75% etc.); Jupiter in lagna can cancel only **75%**; a large inauspicious weight remains (the teacher mentioned "175%" remaining; he said it is not a mathematical formula).

> [!CAUTION] Scope rule for this course (important)
> - **For now (prediction part): use only the 100% aspects and the kendrīya prabhāva.** The other pādas (25/50/75%) were taught only so the students understand — e.g., why the Sun and Saturn also matter in the example above.
> - Other houses will be examined only when mastery grows. Use the pāda detail **only gradually**, after full confidence in 100% aspects.
> - **Q (Student):** "Promise" from D1 and fulfilment from the vargas — so for the promise from the lagna chart we use only 100% aspects and kendrīya prabhāva? **A (Teacher):** Yes, only these. *Very good.*

## 14. Principles compilation (what was taught in this class)

### A. Nature of signs in houses
1. In **any lagna**, the kendra houses (1, 4, 7, 10) all hold signs of the **same nature**; 2, 5, 8, 11 all fixed; 3, 6, 9, 12 all dual.
2. Each **trikoṇa** contains all **three natures** (movable, fixed, dual).
3. When kendra and trikoṇa relate, "positive and negative go together" and results are better *(explained later)*.

### B. House significations
4. Use **one word** per house: 1 tan, 2 dhan, 3 bal, 4 sukh, 5 santān, 6 śatru, 7 jāyā, 8 āyu, 9 bhāgya, 10 karma, 11 lābha/āya, 12 vyaya. The single word is the basis for later expansion.
5. 1st: body, looks, physical health. 2nd: family and speech. 3rd: strength, younger siblings, helpers, younger/same-age friends. 4th: all material comfort and mother's love. 11th: gains and older friends.
6. Elder siblings are not bal; they are guru-like.

### C. Derived houses (bhāvāt bhāvam)
7. Count from a house to the same house from it; it also speaks of the same matter (5th from 5th for children, 4th from 4th, 8th from 8th, 10th from 10th), but not equal to the original house.
8. Do it **once only**.
9. The **12th from a house** is the loss/expense of that house.
10. 2nd and 7th are **māraka**.
11. For the **mother**, treat the 4th as lagna: loss = 12th from it; her happiness = 4th from it; her strength (siblings) = 3rd from it; her siblings (maternal uncle) = 6th house.

### D. Classification of houses
12. **Kendra:** 1, 4, 7, 10 (śubha bhāva). **Trikoṇa:** 1, 5, 9 (yogakāraka, stronger than kendra).
13. **Aśubha:** 3, 6, 11. **Duḥkha (trika):** 6, 8, 12. **Sama [unclear in transcript]:** 2, 8, 12. **Māraka:** 2, 7. **Upacaya:** 3, 6, 10, 11.
14. The **lord of an inauspicious house does not always give inauspicious results** (explained in prediction).
15. Excess is to be avoided (*ati sarvatra varjayet*) — hence even 3rd and 11th are treated as inauspicious.
16. Check a planet **from lagna and from its own house** to see kendra/trikoṇa placement.
17. Kendra sets: 1-4-7-10, 2-5-8-11, 3-6-9-12; trikoṇa sets: 1-5-9, 2-6-10, 3-7-11, 4-8-12.

### E. Result-giving method
18. **Result = bhāva + dātā graha.** Keep the circle smallest.
19. For the **bhāva**: look at the bhāva, bhāveśa, sthira kāraka. For the **dātā graha**: the planet alone.
20. The **dātā graha is the cara kāraka**, chosen by matching the nature of the **question** to the planet (father → Sun; husband for a woman → Jupiter; wife for a man → Venus; speech → Mercury; fortune → Jupiter).
21. **Sthira kārakas (fixed):** 1 Sun, 2 Jupiter, 3 Mars, 4 Moon, 5 Jupiter, 6 Mars, 7 Venus, 8 Saturn, 9 Jupiter, 10 Mercury, 11 Jupiter, 12 Saturn.
22. The kāraka is made by **matching the house's characteristics with the planet's**; sthira kārakas are fixed, dātā graha changes.
23. A **planet that is kāraka of two houses** gives the results of both, and the two connect.
24. The **rule is held unbreakable**: results come from the bhāva and the dātā graha, whatever number of years one studies.

### F. Dṛṣṭi (aspects)
25. Every planet aspects the 7th. Mars also 4th and 8th; Jupiter 5th and 9th; Rahu–Ketu 5th and 9th; Saturn 3rd and 10th.
26. **Natural benefic aspects are auspicious; natural malefic aspects are inauspicious**; temporary lordship/placement does not change this.
27. **Mercury:** benefic alone, malefic with malefics (e.g., in Budha-Āditya yoga). **Moon:** inauspicious within 72° ahead/behind the Sun; otherwise auspicious.
28. Sun, Mars, Saturn are undisputed malefics; Jupiter, Venus undisputed benefics; each malefic harms in line with its nature.
29. An aspect **activates the house first, then gives results**, which may stay as they were or be transformed.
30. An aspect works on **the house and the planets in it**, not on the bhāveśa.
31. Aspects hit **living significations** (mother, siblings, family, maternal uncle, spouse, in-laws, father, colleagues) more strongly.
32. Retrograde does not change an aspect; no daśā is needed; Rahu–Ketu aspect each other.
33. **Pāda strengths:** 7th (all) 100%; Mars 4th/8th 100%; Jupiter 5th/9th 100%; Saturn 3rd/10th 100%. Others: 3rd/10th (non-Saturn) 25%; 5th/9th (non-Jupiter) 50%; 4th/8th (non-Mars) 75%. Every planet thus has seven aspects.
34. **Kartarī:** only the **immediately adjacent** houses count; the degree within the house makes no difference.
35. Mixed influence on a house is real (e.g., Mars 100% with Sun/Saturn 75%); one benefic can cancel only a portion; this is not a mathematical formula.

### G. Kendrīya prabhāva
36. A planet influences the **1st, 4th, 7th and 10th from itself** (own house, 4th at 75%, 7th at 100%, 10th at 25%). A malefic in a kendra disturbs its kendras; a benefic improves them.
37. "10th effect" is a partial version; the teacher teaches the full kendrīya prabhāva.
38. Saying: *Jupiter in lagna removes a lakh doshas; Saturn in lagna loads them.*

### H. Teacher's scope rules (what to apply now)
39. For the "promise" in D1: use **only 100% aspects and kendrīya prabhāva**; the vargas show fulfilment.
40. Apply the pāda detail only after confidence in the 100% aspects; trine effect belongs to transits.
41. Do not bring in what has not been taught (e.g., 12th from the 8th for longevity loss, ṣaḍbala, aspect-effect questions).
42. Questions on effect/prediction are answered when the effect is taught; in the exercises only the aspect application is learnt.

### I. Rules from the previous-class follow-up
43. The fault of the 2nd/8th/12th lord is cancelled when that lord is the lagneśa or a friend of the lagneśa (or in a trikoṇa, as the student recited).
44. Friendship is seen from the **mūlatrikoṇa** sign; houses 2, 4, 5, 8, 9, 12 from there are friend houses (as stated by a student and accepted).
45. Planets **want to give** each other the same results they receive; ability is a separate matter.

### J. Stated by students, not confirmed by the teacher

## Teacher's introduction (method statements)

> [!IMPORTANT] What the teacher said before the chart
> 1. For **any** topic (career, health, finance) the first thing to understand is the person's **nature**. That is why nature is the first practice topic; a few practice classes will each add something new.
> 2. Knowing the nature lets the astrologer give a **remedy** and understand where a problem comes from (e.g. if marriage has problems, the difficulty comes from this side). Everyone's chart has Rahu, Ketu, Saturn, Mars somewhere; the basic issue is the nature. Today's young people have more problems (career, marriage, relationships) than the older generation; the reason is thinking: "neither of the two will bow down", and at a job "we do not want to bow to anyone".
> 3. Nature is not seen from one house. The basics: **lagna, lagna lord, the influences on lagna (aspect, or a planet in lagna), the placement of the lagna lord and the influences on it, and the Moon**. Beyond that, **all twelve houses** are used, because the twelve houses are the parts of one's own life: body, habits, home and family, way of working, courage, health, thinking, and even hidden facts ("Scorpio is in everyone's chart, so everyone has hidden facts").
> 4. Put all houses together and write **two lines** about the nature.
> 5. Class practice: do not repeat what another person already said (e.g. if someone said "Jupiter is in lagna", nobody needs to repeat it). Give a **three-to-four line** answer, with the **reasoning** for each trait stated.

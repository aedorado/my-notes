# Part 2: Principles Compendium

## 1. Reading method and sequence

**P1. Three checks, and stay on the question**
- **Principle:** Before reading, keep three things in mind: deśa-kāla-paristhiti, the client's nature, and the client's question. Discuss only what the question needs, and do not drift from marriage into health.
- **Stated by:** Teacher
- **Shown in:** [[#Chart 1]], [[#Chart 2]]
- **Exceptions / cancellations:** none stated
- **Teacher's reasoning:** none stated

**P2. Age and place decide what counts as delay**
- **Principle:** Whether a marriage is "delayed" depends on place and time, e.g. 20 years may be delay in one place, 35 not in another.
- **Stated by:** Teacher
- **Shown in:** [[#Chart 1]]
- **Exceptions / cancellations:** none stated
- **Teacher's reasoning:** none stated

**P3. Ask the client for context**
- **Principle:** Ask about family, proposals, breakups, status, how the match was found, and health, because these show the cause. The chart can also catch the client's problem, but ask first.
- **Stated by:** Teacher
- **Shown in:** [[#Chart 1]], [[#Chart 2]], [[#Chart 3]], [[#Chart 4]]
- **Exceptions / cancellations:** none stated
- **Teacher's reasoning:** "Health sometimes becomes a reason for not marrying." The client's answers confirm what a planet shows.

**P4. Do not say "no marriage" when Guru aspects the 7th**
- **Principle:** If Guru aspects the 7th, say "afflicted", not "no marriage yoga". The "not there" verdict is never given.
- **Stated by:** Teacher
- **Shown in:** [[#Chart 1]], [[#Chart 2]]
- **Exceptions / cancellations:** none stated
- **Teacher's reasoning:** none stated

**P5. Where trouble and divorce show**
- **Principle:** For divorce look at the 5th, 8th and 10th along with the 7th. The most afflicted house in the chart shows divorce. In a male chart look at planets connected with Śukra.
- **Stated by:** Teacher

- **Teacher's reasoning:** none stated

- **Principle:** The happiness of any house is read from its 4th. The 7th gives marriage; the 10th shows its happiness. Read where the daśā goes from the 10th and from Śukra.

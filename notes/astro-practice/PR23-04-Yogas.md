> 2. Which yoga is the best?
> 3. What is the ultimate use of yogas in our life?
> 4. What is happiness?

# Part 2: Principles Compendium

## 1. Reading method and sequence

**P1. What a yoga is**
- **Principle:** Two or more planets in conjunction, in a particular house, with a particular mutual relation, which affects the jātaka's life, is a yoga. Students add aspect (dṛṣṭi) and exchange (parivartana) as yoga makers.
- **Stated by:** Anju G. ✅ (conjunction); Nanda ✅ (aspect, exchange; teacher prompted "exchange")
- **Shown in:** Opening, [[#Chart 1]]
- **Exceptions / cancellations:** none stated
- **Teacher's reasoning:** none stated

**P2. Read the yoga for the client's question**
- **Principle:** Read yogas according to the question: career → career houses, children → 5th, father → 9th, marriage → 7th. The same yoga reads differently from each house.
- **Stated by:** Teacher
- **Shown in:** [[#Chart 1]], [[#Chart 2]]
- **Exceptions / cancellations:** none stated
- **Teacher's reasoning:** "I will be happy only if my child is happy", so the native's own yoga alone is not enough.

**P3. Two views for a conjunction in marriage**
- **Principle:** For a conjunction, give one prediction from the lagna lord's view and one from the 7th lord's view, with the 7th as target. The easy way is to make the 7th the lagna.
- **Stated by:** Teacher
- **Shown in:** [[#Chart 2]]
- **Exceptions / cancellations:** none stated
- **Teacher's reasoning:** none stated

**P4. Conjunction only, when the exercise is conjunction**
- **Principle:** In an exercise on conjunction, do not bring in aspect.
- **Stated by:** Teacher ✏️ (correcting Anju Aditya and Dravin)
- **Shown in:** [[#Chart 2]]
- **Exceptions / cancellations:** This was scoped to that exercise. Aspects were used in [[#Chart 1]] (Śani–Sūrya).
- **Teacher's reasoning:** none stated

**P5. Whole chart, and the daśā pointer**
- **Principle:** A 10th question needs the whole chart, though the 10th alone may do. If Ketu daśā runs, look at the 12th. If Candra–Guru daśā runs, look at lagna. With no daśā, give general career points.
- **Stated by:** Teacher (applied)
- **Shown in:** [[#Chart 1]]
- **Exceptions / cancellations:** none stated
- **Teacher's reasoning:** none stated

**P6. Sign, planet and event are read separately**
- **Principle:** The effect of a sign, happiness from a sign, and a particular event are shown from different houses. The planet there and the sign there are read separately.
- **Stated by:** Teacher
- **Shown in:** [[#Chart 1]]
- **Exceptions / cancellations:** none stated
- **Teacher's reasoning:** none stated

**P7. 6th from 6th, struggle in every house**

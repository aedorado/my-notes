# Part 2: Principles Compendium

## 1. Reading method and sequence

**P1. Family background first**
- **Principle:** Before predicting, learn the person's family and place (deśa-kāla-paristhiti). If Śani (karma kāraka) sits in the 9th (father's house), ask about the father's work. Include the local education system.
- **Stated by:** Teacher
- **Shown in:** [[#Chart 2]], [[#Chart 4]], [[#Chart 5]]
- **Exceptions / cancellations:** none stated
- **Teacher's reasoning:** Śani in the father's house asks whether the person works with the family.

**P2. Dasha first, mahādaśā first for career**
- **Principle:** The dasha has the main effect. For career look at the mahādaśā first, not the antardaśā each time. The sign and placement of the dasha lord matter most.
- **Stated by:** Teacher
- **Shown in:** [[#Chart 1]], [[#Chart 2]], [[#Chart 3]], [[#Chart 5]]
- **Exceptions / cancellations:** none stated
- **Teacher's reasoning:** "You do not change career in every antardaśā."

**P3. Apply all rules, not one**
- **Principle:** Apply the rules one by one. Do not decide from a single rule.
- **Stated by:** Teacher
- **Shown in:** [[#Chart 1]], [[#Chart 2]], [[#Chart 6]]
- **Exceptions / cancellations:** none stated
